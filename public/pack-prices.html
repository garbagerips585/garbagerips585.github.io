The bigger box is not the cheaper pack

The received wisdom is that the bigger the box the cheaper the pack. Of the 22 sets here that sell a pack both loose and inside something bigger, that holds 9 times and fails 13 times: buying single packs one at a time is the cheapest pack in Chaos Rising, Journey Together and 11 more. It is not a rule that can be applied from the outside: on the 2 priciest the cheapest pack is bought loose. The only way to know is to look at the set you are actually buying, and that is what the table below is.

The pack that wins

Where buying one pack is the cheap way

We do not hold a record of which sets are still being printed, so we are not going to tell you why. The Elite Trainer Box

What the ETB premium looks like

All 22 sets on this page sell an Elite Trainer Box, and in not one of them is it the cheapest pack. In 12 of the 22 it is the dearest thing on the row. Measured against the cheapest pack in its own set it runs a median of 1.58x.

That gap is not the whole story and this page will not pretend it is. An Elite Trainer Box also carries sleeves, dice, a promo card, dividers and the box itself, and we have no source for what any of that is worth, so we do not subtract a made up number from the price to make the comparison look better. If you want the sleeves, the premium may be the cheapest sleeves you will buy. If you want packs, there is a cheaper way to get them in every set on this page.

Before you buy anything

What this page will not tell you

  • Whether the cards inside are worth the price. That calculation needs pull rates, and The Pokemon Company does not publish them, so nobody quoting you one has them either. What we do have is 280 openings of these sets on camera with the results written down, which is observed and not the same thing.
  • What you will pay at a shop. Market price is what copies have been selling for online. A counter in Rochester or a table at a card show is its own market, sometimes under this and sometimes over, and there is no feed for either.
  • Anything about half boxes, blisters, tins or collection boxes. They are in the sync and on the set guides with their full prices, but their pack counts are either variable or not in our data, so they are not divided here. A per-pack figure over a guessed pack count is worse than no figure.
  • Which set to open. Cheapest is not best and this is not a recommendation. It is a price list.

Prices are TCGplayer market prices, pulled by scripts/sync-products.mjs and read August 16, 2026. The pack counts are our own per-kind figures for the current era, sourced on how many packs are in it and applied only to sets from the Scarlet & Violet era onward, because they are not what those products held in earlier ones. Every per-pack figure on this page is one of those prices divided by one of those counts, computed when the page was built. No affiliate links, and nothing here is a recommendation to buy anything. Set guides for all 28 of these are under set guides, and what it costs to buy the cards instead of the packs is on cost to complete a set.
