September 16, 2026 in 12 days

30th Celebration

Confirmed date

The anniversary set, and the first Pokemon TCG expansion to launch worldwide on the same day as Japan. Every single card in it is foil, including the basic Energy.

  • A new rarity, Futuristic rare, debuting on Mewtwo ex and Mew ex
  • 30 different Pikachu illustrations by 30 illustrators
  • A 30 card Classic Collection of reprints, numbered outside the main set
  • Packs are 5 foil cards plus a foil basic Energy

128 cards in the set, going by the numbering on the cards themselves.

Preorders are running hot. The Figure Collection [Mew] has a $29.99 list price and is going for $125.59, which is 4.2 times retail, months before it is on a shelf. Prices usually fall after release.

November 6, 2026 in 2 months

Mega Evolution: Delta Reign ME06

Confirmed date

The sixth English Mega Evolution set, headlined by Mega Rayquaza ex coming back to the card game. The Pokemon Company has published the whole card breakdown for this one, which is unusual this far out, and it comes with a new kind of Stadium card that only does anything as a pair.

  • Over 135 cards, more than 20 of them Trainer cards, and over 35 cards with special illustrations
  • Four new Mega Evolution Pokemon ex: Mega Rayquaza ex, Mega Golurk ex, Mega Malamar ex and Mega Golisopod ex
  • Split Stadium cards with Legendary in the name. Both halves carry the same name and you need both to play it, so a deck can hold two complete ones at most. Placed side by side they form one extended picture, and a pack holding one holds both of its halves
  • The rest of the breakdown, published by The Pokemon Company itself on August 20, 2026: six Stadium cards making three Legendary Stadiums, six Pokemon ex, 14 illustration rares, 17 ultra rares and six special illustration rares
  • Prereleases start October 24 at participating local game stores, which is The Pokemon Company's own date. It has not announced an end, so the November 1 finish is PokeBeach's
  • Pokemon TCG Live gets the set on November 5, the day before it reaches shelves
