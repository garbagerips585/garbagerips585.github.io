What is coming next

Every announced English Pokemon TCG release, with what is actually in it. Dates say how well known they are, because a confirmed date and a shop's guess are not the same thing and most sites print them identically.

September 16, 2026 in 4 weeks

30th Celebration

Confirmed date

The anniversary set, and the first Pokemon TCG expansion to launch worldwide on the same day as Japan. Every single card in it is foil, including the basic Energy.

  • A new rarity, Futuristic rare, debuting on Mewtwo ex and Mew ex
  • 30 different Pikachu illustrations by 30 illustrators, one guaranteed in every pack
  • A 30 card Classic Collection of reprints, numbered outside the main set
  • Packs are 5 foil cards plus a foil basic Energy

128 cards in the set, going by the numbering on the cards themselves.

Preorders are running hot. The ex Tin [Set of 2] has a $43.98 list price and is going for $299.81, which is 6.8 times retail, months before it is on a shelf. Prices usually fall after release.

November 6, 2026 in 3 months

Mega Evolution: Delta Reign ME06

Confirmed date

The next Mega Evolution set, teased by The Pokemon Company in June. Mega Rayquaza ex comes back to the TCG, with Hoenn flavor running through it.

  • Mega Rayquaza ex returns, as a Double Rare
  • Paired Stadium cards: one artwork split across two Stadiums played together
  • Mega Golisopod ex, Mega Golurk ex and Mega Malamar ex appear in the Japanese set it comes from

Nothing on the calendar right now
