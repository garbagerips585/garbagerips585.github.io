Card shops and where to play

Home / Local scene / Card shops

Where I actually buy, and where you can sit down and play. Real shops around Rochester, New York, run by people who know the hobby. In the hobby these are your LCS, short for local card shop, and around here the counter you buy from and the table you play at are usually the same building, so both are on one page. Buy local when you can: the shop is why the local scene exists.

  • LingSter Games

    West Ridge Road, Rochester, NY

    Deep Pokemon singles and sealed, and the first stop when a set drops.

    • Sealed product
    • Singles
    • New releases
    Where
    1586 West Ridge Rd, Rochester, NY 14615
    Phone
    585-287-6308
    Open
    Wed to Sat 11am to 9:30pm, Sun 11am to 6pm, closed Mon and Tueconfirmed on their own site, August 25, 2026

    lingstergames.com YouTube

  • Millennium Games

    Jefferson Road, Henrietta

    One of the biggest game stores in the area, with a Pokemon section to match and a sanctioned league of its own.

    • Sealed product
    • Singles
    • In store
    • Organized Play
    Where
    1225 Jefferson Road, Suite A17, Rochester, NY 14623
    Phone
    585-427-2190
    Open
    Mon to Sat 10am to 10pm, Sun 10am to 6pmconfirmed on their own site, August 25, 2026

    You can play here

    • League nights, Mondays at 6pm
    • Trade days, about once a quarter
    • Prerelease events for new sets

    Straight from the store, by email on August 27, 2026: league nights every Monday at 6pm, and trade days roughly once a quarter with no fixed schedule. Their Delta Reign prerelease is penciled in for October 25 and is not locked yet; it goes on their own calendar once it is. Their Play! Pokemon league page still reads Inactive, which is their listing being stale rather than the league being off.

    shop.millenniumgames.com League page Events Singles

  • Just Games

    Panorama Plaza, Penfield

    The flagship game store in Rochester, NY, and the biggest of these. Singles, sealed and a proper events room. Through 2025 it had the busiest Pokemon calendar in the area; that calendar has gone quiet, so read the note below before you plan a trip around it.

    • Singles
    • Sealed product
    • In store
    Where
    1601 Penfield Rd, Suite 34, Rochester, NY 14625
    Phone
    585-586-0586
    Open
    Mon to Sat noon to 9pm, Sun noon to 6pmconfirmed on their own site, August 25, 2026

    Their Pokemon events have gone quiet, and it is their own three sources that say so, all read August 26, 2026. The Pokemon page this listing used to link has not been posted to since December 3, 2025 and carries nothing from 2026. Their live events calendar, covering late August to mid October 2026, lists no Pokemon at all: Magic, Lorcana, Star Wars Unlimited, 40K, D&D and Dice Throne, and no Pokemon. And their Play! Pokemon league reads Inactive. They ran prereleases, League Challenges and Cups, a Gym Leader Challenge season and Learn to Play sessions right through 2025, so this may be a gap rather than an ending. Call the counter before you drive out.

    justgamesrochester.com Events

  • Legacy Games

    Ridge Road, Webster

    The Webster one, and the furthest east of these. They run a separate singles storefront alongside the main site, so you can see which Pokemon singles are actually in the case before you make the drive. They buy cards too.

    • Singles
    • Buylist
    • Organized Play
    • Prereleases
    • In store
    Where
    1028 Ridge Rd, Suite 112, Webster, NY 14580
    Phone
    585-866-4263
    Open
    Mon to Sat 11am to 9:30pm, Sun noon to 7pmconfirmed on their own site, August 25, 2026

    You can play here

    • Pokemon TCG League, Tuesdays 4:30pm to 9:30pm
    • Casual play, Mondays
    • Release events for new Pokemon sets
    • Learn to play events, held regularly

    The Monday casual play, the release events and the learn to play sessions come from the store directly, by email on August 27, 2026. The Tuesday league is from their own event pages: $5 to enter and it comes back as store credit, with a Pokemon prize pack on entry. Their calendar only publishes about two weeks ahead, so it shows one Tuesday at a time rather than a weekly series.

    legacygamestore.com Events Singles YouTube Instagram Facebook Discord

  • Great Lakes Gaming

    South Clinton Avenue, downtown

    Not a shop front. It is a gaming lounge on the 22nd floor of an office tower downtown that sells Pokemon packs, Elite Trainer Boxes and booster boxes at the counter and gives Wednesday night over to Pokemon.

    • Sealed product
    • Organized Play
    • In store
    Where
    100 S Clinton Ave, Suite 22, Rochester, NY 14604
    Phone
    585-604-9626
    Open
    Wed, Thu and Sun 5pm to 10pm, Fri and Sat 5pm to midnight. Closed Mon and Tue.confirmed on their own site, August 26, 2026

    You can play here

    • Wooper Wednesday, Pokemon tournaments from 6:30pm
    • Trading card game and video game, plus Pokemon Go and Unite

    The Wednesday time is off their own trading card games page. Being on the 22nd floor, it is an elevator and a parking garage rather than a door off the street, so leave a few extra minutes.

    glgaminglounges.com Events

  • WeTheHobby

    Arlington Street, Neighborhood of the Arts

    Mostly sports cards, and better known for breaking live online than for a Pokemon singles case. The Arlington Street building is a real shop and lounge you can walk into though, and there is a Pokemon league in it every week.

    • Organized Play
    • In store
    Where
    23 Arlington St, Rochester, NY 14607
    Phone
    585-730-5819
    Open
    Mon to Wed noon to 6pm, Thu and Fri noon to 8pm, Sat 11am to 8pm, Sun 11am to 6pmconfirmed on their own site, August 25, 2026

    You can play here

    • Pokemon Weekly League, Thursdays

    Their own events calendar carries the league on every Thursday of the current month, but does not publish a start time. Check the calendar or ask when you walk in.

    wethehobby.com Events

  • GI Cards and Collectibles

    Geneva, in the Finger Lakes

    A Geneva card shop that also runs the Sports Cards and Collectibles Show at del Lago, which is where most people around here will meet them. Their own store is heaviest on Magic and sealed sports, and their shows are billed as Pokemon, Magic and One Piece as well.

    • Sealed product
    • Runs card shows

    gicardsny.com YouTube Instagram Facebook Discord Whatnot X

Play! Pokemon league schedules change constantly and go quiet between seasons. Call the shop before you drive.

Looking for a one off rather than a weekly night? The card show calendar has every show coming up around Rochester, NY, Buffalo and Syracuse.

Making a day of it? A plate is the other thing this city is known for, and the Garbage Plate page has the sourced history, a diagram of what is actually on one, and 11 places around here that serve one.

Not everyone selling cards around here has a storefront. Local vendors are the breakers and sellers we buy from without one, and local creators is who else is filming Pokemon in Rochester, NY, Buffalo and Syracuse. Everything local in one place is the short version of all of it, with the shows, the shops and the plate counted.

Addresses and phone numbers were last checked on August 15, 2026; opening hours carry their own date and source on each shop. Shops move and change their hours, so call ahead if you are making a trip of it.
