Card shops and where to play

Where I actually buy, and where you can sit down and play. Real shops around Rochester, New York, run by people who know the hobby. Round here the counter you buy from and the table you play at are usually the same building, so both are on one page. Buy local when you can: the shop is why the local scene exists.

  • Just Games

    Panorama Plaza, Penfield

    Rochester's flagship game store and the one with the busiest Pokemon calendar. Prereleases every set, a learn to play session for anyone starting out, and League Challenges and Cups through the year.

    • Singles
    • Sealed product
    • Organised play
    • Prereleases
    Where
    1601 Penfield Rd, Suite 34, Rochester, NY 14625
    Phone
    585-586-0586
    Open
    Mon to Sat noon to 9pm, Sun noon to 6pm

    You can play here

    • Prerelease weekends for every new set
    • League Challenge and League Cup events
    • Gym Leader Challenge, a casual format away from standard
    • Learn to Play sessions with loaner decks

    They run a regular league but do not publish a fixed weekly time, so check the events calendar or ring the counter.

    justgamesrochester.com →

  • Millennium Games

    Jefferson Road, Henrietta

    One of the biggest game stores in the area, with a Pokemon section to match and a sanctioned league of its own.

    • Sealed product
    • Singles
    • In store
    • Organised play
    Where
    1225 Jefferson Road, Suite A17, Rochester, NY 14623
    Phone
    585-427-2190

    You can play here

    • Sanctioned Play! Pokemon TCG league

    Their official Play! Pokemon league page currently reads Inactive with no sessions scheduled, while other listings still describe a Monday league night. Ring them before you go.

    shop.millenniumgames.com → Official league page →

  • LingSter Games

    Rochester, NY

    Deep Pokemon singles and sealed, and the first stop when a set drops.

    • Sealed product
    • Singles
    • New releases

    lingstergames.com →

Play! Pokemon league schedules change constantly and go quiet between seasons. Ring the shop before you drive.

Looking for a one off rather than a weekly night? The card show calendar has every show coming up around Rochester, Buffalo and Syracuse.
