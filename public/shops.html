Card shops and where to play

Where I actually buy, and where you can sit down and play. Real shops around Rochester, New York, run by people who know the hobby. Round here the counter you buy from and the table you play at are usually the same building, so both are on one page. Buy local when you can: the shop is why the local scene exists.

  • Just Games

    Panorama Plaza, Penfield

    Rochester's flagship game store and the one with the busiest Pokemon calendar. Prereleases every set, a learn to play session for anyone starting out, and League Challenges and Cups through the year.

    • Singles
    • Sealed product
    • Organized Play
    • Prereleases
    Where
    1601 Penfield Rd, Suite 34, Rochester, NY 14625
    Phone
    585-586-0586
    Open
    Mon to Sat noon to 9pm, Sun noon to 6pm

    You can play here

    • Prerelease weekends for every new set
    • League Challenge and League Cup events
    • Gym Leader Challenge, a casual format away from standard
    • Learn to Play sessions with loaner decks

    They run a regular league but do not publish a fixed weekly time, so check the events calendar or call the counter.

    justgamesrochester.com →

  • Millennium Games

    Jefferson Road, Henrietta

    One of the biggest game stores in the area, with a Pokemon section to match and a sanctioned league of its own.

    • Sealed product
    • Singles
    • In store
    • Organized Play
    Where
    1225 Jefferson Road, Suite A17, Rochester, NY 14623
    Phone
    585-427-2190
    Open
    Mon to Sat 10am to 10pm, Sun 10am to 6pm

    You can play here

    • Sanctioned Play! Pokemon TCG league

    Their official Play! Pokemon league page currently reads Inactive with no sessions scheduled, while other listings still describe a Monday league night. Call them before you go.

    shop.millenniumgames.com → Official league page →

  • LingSter Games

    West Ridge Road, Rochester

    Deep Pokemon singles and sealed, and the first stop when a set drops.

    • Sealed product
    • Singles
    • New releases
    Where
    1586 West Ridge Rd, Rochester, NY 14615
    Phone
    585-287-6308
    Open
    Wed to Sat 11am to 9:30pm, Sun 11am to 6pm, closed Mon and Tue

    lingstergames.com →

  • Legacy Games

    Ridge Road, Webster

    The Webster one, and the furthest east of these. They run a separate singles storefront alongside the main site, so you can see which Pokemon singles are actually in the case before you make the drive. They buy cards too.

    • Singles
    • Buylist
    • In store
    Where
    1028 Ridge Rd, Suite 112, Webster, NY 14580
    Phone
    585-866-4263
    Open
    Mon to Sat 11am to 9:30pm, Sun noon to 7pm

    legacygamestore.com →

  • Great Lakes Gaming

    South Clinton Avenue, downtown

    Not a shop front. It is a gaming lounge on the 22nd floor of an office tower downtown that sells Pokemon packs, Elite Trainer Boxes and booster boxes at the counter and gives Wednesday night over to Pokemon.

    • Sealed product
    • Organized Play
    • In store
    Where
    100 S Clinton Ave, Suite 22, Rochester, NY 14604
    Phone
    585-604-9626

    You can play here

    • Wooper Wednesday, Pokemon tournaments from 6:30pm
    • Trading card game and video game, plus Pokemon Go and Unite

    The Wednesday time is off their own trading card games page. Being on the 22nd floor, it is an elevator and a parking garage rather than a door off the street, so leave a few extra minutes.

    glgaminglounges.com →

  • WeTheHobby

    Arlington Street, Neighborhood of the Arts

    Mostly sports cards, and better known for breaking live online than for a Pokemon singles case. The Arlington Street building is a real shop and lounge you can walk into though, and there is a Pokemon league in it every week.

    • Organized Play
    • In store
    Where
    23 Arlington St, Rochester, NY 14607
    Phone
    585-730-5819
    Open
    Mon to Wed noon to 6pm, Thu and Fri noon to 8pm, Sat 11am to 8pm, Sun 11am to 6pm

    You can play here

    • Pokemon Weekly League, Thursdays

    Their own events calendar carries the league on every Thursday of the current month, but does not publish a start time. Check the calendar or ask when you walk in.

    wethehobby.com →

Play! Pokemon league schedules change constantly and go quiet between seasons. Call the shop before you drive.

Looking for a one off rather than a weekly night? The card show calendar has every show coming up around Rochester, Buffalo and Syracuse.

Addresses, phone numbers and opening hours were last checked on August 15, 2026. Shops move and change their hours, so call ahead if you are making a trip of it.
