What is in a collection box?

A themed box built around a character or a pair of them, with promo cards, sometimes an oversize card, and a few packs. The contents vary more than any other product here.

That is what this kind of box usually holds. It is deliberately not stated per set, because the contents have changed between releases and we do not have a per set count we can stand behind. What we do have is what came out of the ones opened here, counted below.
