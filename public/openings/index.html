Every kind of sealed product

What is actually inside each kind of box, what it costs across the sets that sell it, and every one of them opened on this channel. 13 kinds, 323 openings, 464 packs counted.

Single Pack

One booster pack, bought loose off a shelf or a peg.

98 opened • 105 packs counted in 98 of them Pictured: Pitch Black Booster Pack

Elite Trainer Box

An Elite Trainer Box is the big one: a stack of packs, a card box to keep them in, sleeves, dice, damage counters and a status marker.

59 opened • 59 packs counted in 59 of them Pictured: Pitch Black Elite Trainer Box

Booster Bundle

A Booster Bundle is a sleeve of packs and nothing else.

54 opened • 54 packs counted in 54 of them Pictured: Pitch Black Booster Bundle

ex Premium Collection

A collection box built around one ex card, with a promo, an oversize card and a few packs.

48 opened • 64 packs counted in 48 of them Pictured: Mega Greninja ex Premium Collection

Japanese Booster Pack

One booster pack out of a Japanese set, bought loose.

16 opened • 57 packs counted in 16 of them Pictured: Storm Emeralda Booster Pack

Collection Box

A themed box built around a character or a pair of them, with promo cards, sometimes an oversize card, and a few packs.

13 opened • 45 packs counted in 13 of them Pictured: Kingambit Illustration Collection

Tin

A metal tin with a promo card and a few packs.

12 opened • 26 packs counted in 12 of them Pictured: Prismatic Evolutions Mini Tin [Flareon]

Blister

A hanging card with one to three packs and a promo, sold on a peg rather than a shelf.

9 opened • 18 packs counted in 9 of them Pictured: Pitch Black Single Pack Blister [Slowpoke]

Poke Ball Tin

A ball-shaped tin, usually with one or two packs and a coin or promo.

6 opened • 6 packs counted in 6 of them Pictured: Pokemon - Poke Ball Tin - Poke Ball (Q4 2025)

Korean Booster Pack

One booster pack out of a Korean set, bought loose.

4 opened • 7 packs counted in 4 of them

Knock Out Collection

A Knock Out Collection is built around one big ex card: the promo, a playmat or a set of dice depending on the release, and a few packs.

2 opened • 4 packs counted in 2 of them Pictured: Knock Out Collection [Chien-Pao]

Chinese Booster Pack

One booster pack out of a Simplified Chinese set, bought loose.

1 opened • 1 pack counted in 1 of them

UPC

An Ultra Premium Collection is the largest sealed product of a set: a big box with a lot of packs, a metal or oversize card and usually a full art promo.

1 opened • 18 packs counted in 1 of them Pictured: 151 Ultra-Premium Collection

That is all 13 kinds.

Pack counts are what we counted in our own videos, not a figure off a box. Where a product's contents are not in our data, the page says so rather than guess. What each kind is SUPPOSED to cost, which is a different question from what it sells for, is on what sealed product should cost. One product has a guide of its own, because almost nothing is written about it anywhere: the 2026 First Partner Illustration Collection, all 27 promos with a price on each. Prices come from TCGplayer, read August 25, 2026, and so do the product photos: each one is a specific product standing in for its kind, named on the card and named again on the page.
