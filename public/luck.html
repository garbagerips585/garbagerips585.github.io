What has actually come out

Counted from the Hit Card cell of the rip log, which is where the cards that came out are written down by hand, so this is a separate tally from the hit rates above and the two will not line up. A rip counts once per rarity however many cards of that rarity came out of it, so these are counts of rips rather than of cards: 139 tags across 127 rips against the 183 cards the log records, and the biggest single rip is 14 cards under 5 tags. That is on purpose: this band is about how often a rip produces each kind of card, and counting one opening five times would let a single lucky box outweigh a month of them. The cards themselves are listed one by one on the best pulls page. They are totals, not rates: a set that gets opened more will show more of everything. A fuller log makes these counts more complete, not more predictive: they say what came out of the packs we opened, never what will come out of yours.

HOW THIS IS COUNTED. A rip counts only once it has been marked as a hit or not a hit in the rip log, so an untagged video is absent rather than assumed. "Hit" means a card worth keeping, judged by eye, not a fixed rarity threshold. These are observed results from one person opening packs on camera, not official pull rates: The Pokemon Company does not publish those, and anyone who tells you they know them is guessing. Small samples are labeled rather than rounded into confidence. Numbers move as more rips are logged.
