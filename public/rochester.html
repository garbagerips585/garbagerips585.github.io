Why the name

The channel is named after this

A Garbage Plate is two mains on a bed of home fries and macaroni salad with hot sauce poured over the lot, and outside Monroe County almost nobody has heard of it. A rip with nothing in it is a garbage rip. That is the whole joke and it is also the reason a Pokemon channel from here is called what it is.

11 places around Rochester serve one, under 11 different names: Nick Tahou Hots holds the trademark on the words Garbage Plate, so everybody else calls theirs something else. That page carries 11 sources and 11 photographs, and it also names the 5 things nobody could source rather than guessing at them.

The part that is missing

The people, and where this page is short

The shows and the shops above are a real answer to what is here. The two pages about PEOPLE are not one yet: 2 vendors and 2 creators, in a region that fills 61 shows between Aug 30, 2026 and Dec 12, 2027. Those two numbers do not belong on the same page and the fix is not to write more names down, it is to be told them.

Nobody is going on either list who has not been checked, which is the whole reason a local list is worth more than a directory, and it is also why it grows slowly. If you sell at the shows, or you film around here, or you just watch somebody local who deserves the traffic, that is the fastest way this page stops being short.

A shop, a show, a vendor, a creator, or anything else local this page has missed: email it straight to garbagerips585@gmail.com. No form, no sign up, and every listing gets checked before it goes up.
