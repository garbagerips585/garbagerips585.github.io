Quick facts

Scarlet & Violet 101

  • Released March 31, 2023, which was 3 years ago.
  • The set is 198 cards on paper, but there are 258 in total. The extra 60 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 60 of the 258 cards are Ultra Rare or better. That is the slice of the checklist worth pulling.
  • Only 6 Hyper Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Gardevoir ex (Special Illustration Rare), sitting around $71.01 raw.
  • We have ripped 5 videos worth of this set on the channel.
