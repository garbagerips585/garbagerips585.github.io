Same cards, different name

Crimson Haze is Twilight Masquerade

If you have watched these rips and then gone looking for the set in a US store, this is the one you want. Crimson Haze is the Korean printing behind English Twilight Masquerade.

    Twilight Masquerade

    226 cards • May 24, 2024

    The English release

    Read the Twilight Masquerade guide

That is the symbol on an English Twilight Masquerade card, printed at the bottom beside the collector number. It is not the mark on the Korean cards in these rips: the two printings carry their own.

A partial match. A smaller Japanese subset whose cards were spread across more than one English set rather than becoming one of its own, so the English match is partial rather than one to one. Checked against the checklists rather than against an article: of the 51 Crimson Haze cards we hold an English name for, 49 appear in the Twilight Masquerade checklist. The next closest English set is Stellar Crown with 6. The Korean release date, May 24, 2024, is TCGdex's, read August 14, 2026.
