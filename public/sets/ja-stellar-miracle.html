Same cards, different name

Stellar Miracle is Stellar Crown

If you have watched these rips and then gone looking for the set in a US shop, this is the one you want. Stellar Miracle is the Japanese printing behind English Stellar Crown.

  • 🇯🇵 Japanese • SV7

    The one on this page, checklist below

  • English • SV7

    Stellar Crown

    175 cards • September 13, 2024

    Out 2 months later

    Read the Stellar Crown guide

That is the symbol on an English Stellar Crown card, printed at the bottom beside the collector number. It is not the mark on the Japanese cards in these rips: the two printings carry their own.

The Japanese set behind Stellar Crown, out about eight weeks earlier.
