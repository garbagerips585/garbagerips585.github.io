Same cards, different name

Abyss Eye is Pitch Black

If you have watched these rips and then gone looking for the set in a US shop, this is the one you want. Abyss Eye is the Japanese printing behind English Pitch Black.

  • 🇯🇵 Japanese • M5

    Abyss Eye

    アビスアイ

    118 cards • May 22, 2026

    The one on this page, checklist below

  • English • ME5

    Pitch Black

    120 cards • July 17, 2026

    Out 2 months later

    Read the Pitch Black guide

That is the symbol on an English Pitch Black card, printed at the bottom beside the collector number. It is not the mark on the Japanese cards in these rips: the two printings carry their own.

Released May 22, 2026, about two months ahead of the English Pitch Black. Same set, same chase lineup, Japanese print run.

Card for card

How close is it to Pitch Black?

"Same set" is nearly true and worth checking rather than taking on trust. Here is every rarity in both printings, side by side. The gold rows are the ones that match.

3 of the 8 rarities hold exactly the same number of cards in both printings: 1 Mega Hyper Rare, 6 Special Illustration Rares and 18 Ultra Rares. English Pitch Black is 2 cards bigger, 120 against 118.

Both counts are the rarities on the two checklists, from TCGdex, read August 16, 2026. A rarity is only counted where TCGdex labels it, so this table appears on a guide only when both sides label every card. It says nothing about how often any of them turn up in a pack, because nobody outside The Pokemon Company knows that.
