Quick facts

When did White Flare come out, and how big is it?

  • Released July 18, 2025, which was 14 months ago.
  • The set is 86 cards on paper, but there are 173 in total. The extra 87 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 87 of the 173 cards sit at a chase rarity, which is every tier tagged CHASE in the rarity breakdown below. That is what the checklist holds, not how often any of it turns up.
  • Only 7 Special Illustration Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Victini (Black White Rare), sitting around $520 raw, and $1,279 in a PSA 10.
  • We have ripped 3 videos worth of this set on the channel.
  • White Flare has a twin. Black Bolt came out the same day and is built to the same shape, down to a rarity the two of them share and nothing else on this site has: Black White Rare, two cards apiece.
  • 70 of the 173 cards are Illustration Rares. Nothing else on this site is close except Black Bolt at 69; the next highest anywhere is 36.

That PSA 10 figure is graded sales data from pricecharting.com, read October 2, 2026. The raw figure beside it is pricecharting.com's price guide value for an ungraded copy, which is a different measurement.

What is actually rare

White Flare secret rares and rarity breakdown

White Flare has 87 cards numbered past the printed set, #087 to #173, against a printed total of 86. That is what collectors mean by a secret rare: the number runs past the total printed beside it. Each one's printed rarity is on the ladder below.
