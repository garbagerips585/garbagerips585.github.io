Quick facts

White Flare 101

  • Released July 18, 2025, which was 13 months ago.
  • The set is 86 cards on paper, but there are 173 in total. The extra 87 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 87 of the 173 cards sit at a chase rarity, which is every tier highlighted in the rarity breakdown below. That is what the checklist holds, not how often any of it turns up.
  • Only 7 Special Illustration Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Victini (Black White Rare), sitting around $551 raw, and $1,474 in a PSA 10.
  • White Flare has a twin. Black Bolt came out the same day and is built to the same shape, down to a rarity the two of them share and nothing else on this site has: Black White Rare, two cards apiece.
  • 70 of the 173 cards are Illustration Rares. Nothing else on this site is close except Black Bolt at 69; the next highest anywhere is 36.

That PSA 10 figure is graded sales data from pricecharting.com, read August 13, 2026. The raw figure beside it is pricecharting.com's price guide value for an ungraded copy, which is a different measurement read on a different day.

Anything whose pack count is not in our data gets no per-pack figure rather than a guessed one, which is why blisters, tins and collection boxes have none, and why sets from before 2023 have none either: our counts are sourced for the current era and were not the same in earlier ones.
