Quick facts

Stellar Crown 101

  • Released September 13, 2024, which was 22 months ago.
  • The set is 142 cards on paper, but there are 175 in total. The extra 33 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 36 of the 175 cards are Ultra Rare or better. That is the slice of the checklist worth pulling.
  • Only 3 Hyper Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Squirtle (Illustration Rare), sitting around $119 raw.
