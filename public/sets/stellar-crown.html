Quick facts

Stellar Crown 101

  • Released September 13, 2024, which was 23 months ago.
  • The set is 142 cards on paper, but there are 175 in total. The extra 33 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 36 of the 175 cards sit at a chase rarity, which is every tier highlighted in the rarity breakdown below. That is what the checklist holds, not how often any of it turns up.
  • Only 3 Hyper Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Squirtle (Illustration Rare), sitting around $111 raw, and $390 in a PSA 10.
  • The two most expensive cards in Stellar Crown are a Squirtle and a Bulbasaur, both Illustration Rares, and both are worth more than every Special Illustration Rare in the set. The tier above is not always where the money is.

That PSA 10 figure is graded sales data from pricecharting.com, read August 22, 2026. The raw figure beside it is pricecharting.com's price guide value for an ungraded copy, which is a different measurement read on a different day.
