Quick facts

Pokémon GO 101

  • Released July 1, 2022, which was 4 years ago.
  • The set is 78 cards on paper, but there are 88 in total. The extra 10 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 3 of the 88 cards are Ultra Rare or better. That is the slice of the checklist worth pulling.
  • The chase card is Mewtwo V (Rare Ultra), sitting around $61.12 raw.
  • We have ripped 12 videos worth of this set on the channel.
