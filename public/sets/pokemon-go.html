Where the value sits

Where the money is

Buy one copy of every card in Pokémon GO at market price and you would spend $388.17. Half of it sits in 4 cards. The other 84 come to $190.40 between them, which is $2.27 a card.

In plain terms: a handful of cards carry the set and everything else is bulk. That is normal, it is true of nearly every modern set, and it is worth knowing before you buy a box hoping to "get your money back".

Quick facts

Pokémon GO 101

  • Released July 1, 2022, which was 4 years ago.
  • The set is 78 cards on paper, but there are 88 in total. The extra 10 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 3 of the 88 cards are Ultra Rare or better. That is the slice of the checklist worth pulling.
  • The chase card is Mewtwo V (Rare Ultra), sitting around $61.12 raw.
  • We have ripped 12 videos worth of this set on the channel.

Prices are TCGplayer market and lowest-listing prices, read on August 12, 2026. They move every day, so treat them as a rough idea and not a quote. Product photos are TCGplayer's. We are not a shop and we do not sell any of this.

Cost per pack is the market price divided by the pack count printed on each card above, so you can check it. Sleeves, dice, decks and promo cards are counted as worth nothing, which flatters every box that includes them. Anything whose pack count is not in our data gets no per-pack figure rather than a guessed one, which is why blisters, tins and collection boxes have none.
