Quick facts

Paldean Fates 101

  • Released January 26, 2024, which was 2 years ago.
  • The set is 91 cards on paper, but there are 245 in total. The extra 154 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 22 of the 245 cards are Ultra Rare or better. That is the slice of the checklist worth pulling.
  • Only 6 Hyper Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Mew ex (Special Illustration Rare), sitting around $1,014 raw.
