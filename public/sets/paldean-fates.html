Quick facts

Paldean Fates 101

  • Released January 26, 2024, which was 2 years ago.
  • The set is 91 cards on paper, but there are 245 in total. The extra 154 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 154 of the 245 cards sit at a chase rarity, which is every tier highlighted in the rarity breakdown below. That is what the checklist holds, not how often any of it turns up.
  • Only 6 Hyper Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Mew ex (Special Illustration Rare), sitting around $880 raw, and $3,027 in a PSA 10.
  • Everything numbered 092 to 223 in this set is a shiny. That is 132 of the 245 cards, more than half the checklist below, which is why this set looks so top heavy next to a normal one.

That PSA 10 figure is graded sales data from pricecharting.com, read August 28, 2026. The raw figure beside it is pricecharting.com's price guide value for an ungraded copy, which is a different measurement read on a different day.
