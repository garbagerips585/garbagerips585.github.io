Quick facts

Black Bolt 101

  • Released July 18, 2025, which was 12 months ago.
  • The set is 86 cards on paper, but there are 172 in total. The extra 86 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 84 of the 172 cards are Ultra Rare or better. That is the slice of the checklist worth pulling.
  • Only 7 Special Illustration Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Victini (Rare), sitting around $607 raw, and $1,537 in a PSA 10.
