Quick facts

When did Black Bolt come out, and how big is it?

  • Released July 18, 2025, which was 15 months ago.
  • The set is 86 cards on paper, but there are 172 in total. The extra 86 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 86 of the 172 cards sit at a chase rarity, which is every tier tagged CHASE in the rarity breakdown below. That is what the checklist holds, not how often any of it turns up.
  • Only 7 Special Illustration Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Victini (Black White Rare), sitting around $613 raw, and $1,163 in a PSA 10.
  • We have 3 rips of this set on the channel.
  • Black Bolt has a twin. White Flare came out the same day and is built to the same shape, down to a rarity the two of them share and nothing else on this site has: Black White Rare, two cards apiece.
  • 69 of the 172 cards are Illustration Rares. Nothing else on this site is close except White Flare at 70; the next highest anywhere is 36.

That PSA 10 figure is graded sales data from pokemonpricetracker.com, read November 14, 2026. The raw figure beside it is the TCGplayer market price for an ungraded copy, which is a different measurement read on a different day.

Prices are TCGplayer market and lowest-listing prices, read on November 14, 2026. They move every day, so treat them as a rough idea and not a quote. Product photos are TCGplayer's. We are not a shop and we do not sell any of this.

A Pokemon Center figure is that exact product's price in Pokemon's own shop, read by hand on August 17, 2026. Pokemon Center is the manufacturer selling its own product, so this site treats its price as the suggested retail price: what sealed Pokemon should cost. The multiple beside it is the market price divided by it, and its name (about retail to 1.15x, a shop's margin to 1.5x, scarcity pricing to 2x, double and up past 2x) is this site's rule of thumb, not a measurement.

Cost per pack is the market price divided by the pack count printed on each card above, so you can check it. Sleeves, dice, decks and promo cards are counted as worth nothing, which flatters every box that includes them. Anything whose pack count is not in our data gets no per-pack figure rather than a guessed one, which is why blisters, tins and collection boxes have none, and why sets from before 2023 have none either: our counts are sourced for the current era and were not the same in earlier ones. What each product has held, and when it changed, is on how many packs are in it.
