Same cards, different name

Mega Symphonia is Mega Evolution

If you have watched these rips and then gone looking for the set in a US store, this is the one you want. Mega Symphonia is the Japanese printing behind English Mega Evolution, which English built by merging it with Mega Brave.

  • 🇯🇵 Japanese • M1S

    Mega Symphonia

    メガシンフォニア

    92 cards • August 1, 2025

    The one on this page, checklist below

  • English • ME1

    Mega Evolution

    188 cards • September 26, 2025

    Out 2 months later

    Read the Mega Evolution guide

That is the symbol on an English Mega Evolution card, printed at the bottom beside the collector number. It is not the mark on the Japanese cards in these rips: the two printings carry their own.

Half of English Mega Evolution. Japan split it into two 92 card sets released the same day, which English merged into one 188 card set.
