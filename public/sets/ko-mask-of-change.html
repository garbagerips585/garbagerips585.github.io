Mask of Change is Twilight Masquerade

If you have watched these rips and then gone looking for the set in a US shop, this is the one you want. Mask of Change is the Korean printing behind English Twilight Masquerade.

That is the symbol on an English Twilight Masquerade card, printed at the bottom beside the collector number. It is not the mark on the Korean cards in these rips: the two printings carry their own.

The Korean printing of the set behind English Twilight Masquerade. Korea got it on June 21, 2024, a month after the English release, which is normal: it follows the Japanese schedule, not the English one.
