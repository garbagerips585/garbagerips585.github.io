Quick facts

Journey Together 101

  • Released March 28, 2025, which was 16 months ago.
  • The set is 159 cards on paper, but there are 190 in total. The extra 31 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 31 of the 190 cards are Ultra Rare or better. That is the slice of the checklist worth pulling.
  • Only 3 Hyper Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Lillie's Clefairy ex (Special Illustration Rare), sitting around $111 raw.
