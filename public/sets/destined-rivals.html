Quick facts

Destined Rivals 101

  • Released May 30, 2025, which was 14 months ago.
  • The set is 182 cards on paper, but there are 244 in total. The extra 62 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 62 of the 244 cards sit at a chase rarity, which is every tier highlighted in the rarity breakdown below. That is what the checklist holds, not how often any of it turns up.
  • Only 6 Hyper Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Team Rocket's Mewtwo ex (Special Illustration Rare), sitting around $482 raw, and $1,127 in a PSA 10.
  • We have ripped 17 videos worth of this set on the channel.
  • 147 of the 244 cards belong to somebody by name. 83 of those are Team Rocket's, and the rest are Cynthia's, Ethan's, Arven's, Marnie's, Misty's, Steven's or an Emcee's.

That PSA 10 figure is graded sales data from pricecharting.com, read August 23, 2026. The raw figure beside it is pricecharting.com's price guide value for an ungraded copy, which is a different measurement read on a different day.
