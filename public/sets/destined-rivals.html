Quick facts

Destined Rivals 101

  • Released May 30, 2025, which was 14 months ago.
  • The set is 182 cards on paper, but there are 244 in total. The extra 62 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 62 of the 244 cards are Ultra Rare or better. That is the slice of the checklist worth pulling.
  • Only 6 Hyper Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Team Rocket's Mewtwo ex (Special Illustration Rare), sitting around $514 raw, and $1,080 in a PSA 10.
