Quick facts

Obsidian Flames 101

  • Released August 11, 2023, which was 3 years ago.
  • The set is 197 cards on paper, but there are 230 in total. The extra 33 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 33 of the 230 cards are Ultra Rare or better. That is the slice of the checklist worth pulling.
  • Only 3 Hyper Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Charizard ex (Special Illustration Rare), sitting around $110 raw.
  • We have ripped 1 video worth of this set on the channel.
