Quick facts

Obsidian Flames 101

  • Released August 11, 2023, which was 3 years ago.
  • The set is 197 cards on paper, but there are 230 in total. The extra 33 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 33 of the 230 cards sit at a chase rarity, which is every tier highlighted in the rarity breakdown below. That is what the checklist holds, not how often any of it turns up.
  • Only 3 Hyper Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Charizard ex (Special Illustration Rare), sitting around $110 raw.
  • We have ripped 1 video worth of this set on the channel.

The stars beside a tier are the ones printed on the card, redrawn from the key in the booklet that ships inside a modern set. Anything whose pack count is not in our data gets no per-pack figure rather than a guessed one, which is why blisters, tins and collection boxes have none, and why sets from before 2023 have none either: our counts are sourced for the current era and were not the same in earlier ones.
