Where the value sits

Where the money is

Buy one copy of every card in Paradox Rift at market price and you would spend $1,240.36. Half of it sits in 14 cards. The other 252 come to $611.92 between them, which is $2.43 a card.

Quick facts

Paradox Rift 101

  • Released November 3, 2023, which was 2 years ago.
  • The set is 182 cards on paper, but there are 266 in total. The extra 84 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 84 of the 266 cards are Ultra Rare or better. That is the slice of the checklist worth pulling.
  • Only 7 Hyper Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Groudon (Illustration Rare), sitting around $127 raw.
  • We have ripped 3 videos worth of this set on the channel.
