Quick facts

Paradox Rift 101

  • Released November 3, 2023, which was 2 years ago.
  • The set is 182 cards on paper, but there are 266 in total. The extra 84 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 84 of the 266 cards sit at a chase rarity, which is every tier highlighted in the rarity breakdown below. That is what the checklist holds, not how often any of it turns up.
  • Only 7 Hyper Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Groudon (Illustration Rare), sitting around $109 raw, and $698 in a PSA 10.
  • We have ripped 3 videos worth of this set on the channel.
  • Plusle and Minun both got an Illustration Rare, both drawn by the same artist, and they sit next to each other at 193 and 194 priced within a few dollars of one another. The set kept them together.
  • The most expensive card here is an Illustration Rare Groudon, and it is worth more than every Special Illustration Rare in the set.

That PSA 10 figure is graded sales data from pricecharting.com, read August 13, 2026. The raw figure beside it is pricecharting.com's price guide value for an ungraded copy, which is a different measurement read on a different day.
