Where the value sits

Where the money is

Buy one copy of every card in Shrouded Fable at market price and you would spend $891.09. Half of it sits in 9 cards. The other 90 come to $440.09 between them, which is $4.89 a card.

In plain terms: a handful of cards carry the set and everything else is bulk. That is normal, it is true of nearly every modern set, and it is worth knowing before you buy a box hoping to "get your money back".

Quick facts

Shrouded Fable 101

  • Released August 2, 2024, which was 2 years ago.
  • The set is 64 cards on paper, but there are 99 in total. The extra 35 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 38 of the 99 cards are Ultra Rare or better. That is the slice of the checklist worth pulling.
  • Only 5 Hyper Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Persian (Illustration Rare), sitting around $72.74 raw.
