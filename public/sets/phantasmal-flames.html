Quick facts

Phantasmal Flames 101

  • Released November 14, 2025, which was 8 months ago.
  • The set is 94 cards on paper, but there are 130 in total. The extra 36 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 36 of the 130 cards are Ultra Rare or better. That is the slice of the checklist worth pulling.
  • Only 1 Mega Hyper Rare exists in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Mega Charizard X ex (Special Illustration Rare), sitting around $717 raw, and $1,962 in a PSA 10.
  • We have ripped 16 videos worth of this set on the channel.
