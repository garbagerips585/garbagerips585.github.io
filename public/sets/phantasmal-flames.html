Quick facts

Phantasmal Flames 101

  • Released November 14, 2025, which was 9 months ago.
  • The set is 94 cards on paper, but there are 130 in total. The extra 36 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 36 of the 130 cards sit at a chase rarity, which is every tier highlighted in the rarity breakdown below. That is what the checklist holds, not how often any of it turns up.
  • Only 1 Mega Hyper Rare exists in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Mega Charizard X ex (Special Illustration Rare), sitting around $750 raw, and $1,941 in a PSA 10.
  • We have ripped 24 videos worth of this set on the channel.
  • Mega Charizard X ex is on four cards here, running from under five dollars up to the most expensive card in the set. Add those four together and they come to about four fifths of what buying one of every card in the set would cost.

That PSA 10 figure is graded sales data from pricecharting.com, read August 22, 2026. The raw figure beside it is pricecharting.com's price guide value for an ungraded copy, which is a different measurement read on a different day.
