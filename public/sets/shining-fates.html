Shining Fates card list and set guide

Shining Fates came out February 19, 2021 with 73 cards: 72 in the printed set and 1 numbered past it, plus the 122 card Shiny Vault, filed as a set of its own. Its most valuable card is Charizard VMAX, Shiny Vault SV107/SV122: $120 for an ungraded copy on pricecharting.com's price guide, read October 5, 2026.

There is a second Shining Fates set, and its prices are on this page. The Shiny Vault is 122 cards numbered SV001 to SV122, out of the same packs and released the same day: shiny cards at their own rarities, 104 of the 122 filed as Shiny Rare. The card databases this site reads file it as a set of its own, so it is not in the 73 counted above, the rarity breakdown or the checklist. It is in the most valuable cards and in where the money is, each of its cards labeled Shiny Vault, and all 122 are priced in their own list.

That is the Shining Fates set symbol. Every card in the set prints it at the bottom, beside the collector number. Holding a card and not sure which set it is?

Quick facts

When did Shining Fates come out, and how big is it?

  • Released February 19, 2021, which was 5 years ago.
  • The set is 72 cards on paper, but there are 73 in total. The extra one is a secret rare numbered past the printed count.
  • 17 of the 73 cards sit at a chase rarity, which is every tier tagged CHASE in the rarity breakdown below. That is what the checklist holds, not how often any of it turns up.
  • The chase card is Charizard VMAX (Shiny Vault SV107/SV122, Shiny Rare VMAX), sitting around $120 raw, and $239 in a PSA 10. On the 73 card checklist alone it is Skyla, at $10.46.
  • We have ripped 2 videos worth of this set on the channel.
  • Three of the cards are Amazing Rares, and they are the only Amazing Rares in any of the 29 English sets on this site.
