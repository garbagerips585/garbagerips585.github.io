Shining Fates

A 2021 set, 73 cards, plus 122 more cards this page does not cover. What is in Shining Fates, what is actually rare in it, and what the cards worth chasing are going for. Skyla leads those 73, and the Shiny Vault is not priced here.

There is a second Shining Fates set and it is not on this page. The Shiny Vault is 122 cards numbered SV001 to SV122, released the same day: shiny cards at their own rarities, 104 of the 122 filed as Shiny Rare. The card databases this site reads file it as a set of its own, so none of it is in the chase cards, on the checklist, or in any total anywhere on this page, and we hold no price for a single one of those 122 cards. Everything below is the 73 card checklist, which is not the whole of what carries the Shining Fates symbol.

That is the Shining Fates set symbol. Every card in the set prints it at the bottom, beside the collector number. Holding a card and not sure which set it is?

Quick facts

Shining Fates 101

  • Released February 19, 2021, which was 5 years ago.
  • The set is 72 cards on paper, but there are 73 in total. The extra one is a secret rare numbered past the printed count.
  • 17 of the 73 cards sit at a chase rarity, which is every tier highlighted in the rarity breakdown below. That is what the checklist holds, not how often any of it turns up.
  • The chase card of the 73 on this checklist is Skyla (Ultra Rare), sitting around $11.21 raw, and $65.00 in a PSA 10. The 122 cards of Shining Fates Shiny Vault are not on it, and this site holds no price for any of them, so there is no honest way for this page to tell you whether one of those beats it.
  • We have ripped 2 videos worth of this set on the channel.
  • Three of the cards are Amazing Rares, and they are the only Amazing Rares in any of the 28 English sets on this site.

That PSA 10 figure is graded sales data from pricecharting.com, read August 22, 2026. The raw figure beside it is pricecharting.com's price guide value for an ungraded copy, which is a different measurement read on a different day.

Cost per pack is the market price divided by the pack count printed on each card above, so you can check it. Sleeves, dice, decks and promo cards are counted as worth nothing, which flatters every box that includes them. Anything whose pack count is not in our data gets no per-pack figure rather than a guessed one, which is why blisters, tins and collection boxes have none, and why sets from before 2023 have none either: our counts are sourced for the current era and were not the same in earlier ones.
