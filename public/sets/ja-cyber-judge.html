Same cards, different name

Cyber Judge is Temporal Forces

If you have watched these rips and then gone looking for the set in a US store, this is the one you want. Cyber Judge is the Japanese printing behind English Temporal Forces, which English built by merging it with Wild Force (ワイルドフォース, SV5K).

That is the symbol on an English Temporal Forces card, printed at the bottom beside the collector number. It is not the mark on the Japanese cards in these rips: the two printings carry their own.

English Temporal Forces is built from two Japanese sets released together: Cyber Judge and Wild Force, 71 cards each.

Where this came from

  • Card scans from TCGdex, and the set details read there September 2, 2026.
  • The checklist, the card names and the rarities on this page are TCGplayer's, read August 25, 2026 from their Pokemon Japan catalog, where this set is filed as SV5M: Cyber Judge. TCGdex lists the set and has published no cards for it: its own record declares 71 cards and carries none of them. Every collector number in the TCGplayer list is written out of 071, which is the same printed set size TCGdex states, and the two catalogs were matched card for card on the collector number before any of this was published.
  • The rarity words here are the Japanese ones. This set's tiers read Art Rare, Super Rare, Special Art Rare and Ultra Rare, which is what is printed on the wrapper. Our other imported guides read TCGdex's anglicized names for the same ladder, so the same card can be called two things across two of these pages. We do not map one onto the other: the two companies publish different names and inventing an equivalence between them is not something this site does.
