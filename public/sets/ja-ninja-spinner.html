Same cards, different name

Ninja Spinner is Chaos Rising

If you have watched these rips and then gone looking for the set in a US store, this is the one you want. Ninja Spinner is the Japanese printing behind English Chaos Rising.

  • 🇯🇵 Japanese • M4

    Ninja Spinner

    ニンジャスピナー

    120 cards • March 13, 2026

    The one on this page, checklist below

  • English • ME4

    Chaos Rising

    122 cards • May 22, 2026

    Out 2 months later

    Read the Chaos Rising guide

That is the symbol on an English Chaos Rising card, printed at the bottom beside the collector number. It is not the mark on the Japanese cards in these rips: the two printings carry their own.

120 cards against Chaos Rising's 122, out roughly ten weeks earlier.

Card for card

How close is it to Chaos Rising?

"Same set" is nearly true and worth checking rather than taking on trust. Here is every rarity in both printings, side by side. The gold rows are the ones that match.

4 of the 8 rarities hold exactly the same number of cards in both printings: 1 Mega Hyper Rare, 6 Special Illustration Rares, 18 Ultra Rares and 38 Commons. English Chaos Rising is 2 cards bigger, 122 against 120.

Both counts are the rarities on the two checklists, from TCGdex, read August 26, 2026. A rarity is only counted where TCGdex labels it, so this table appears on a guide only when both sides label every card. It says nothing about how often any of them turn up in a pack, because nobody outside The Pokemon Company knows that.
