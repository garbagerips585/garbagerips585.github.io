Celebrations

A 2021 set, 25 cards, plus 25 more cards this page does not cover. What is in Celebrations, what is actually rare in it, and what the cards worth chasing are going for. Mew leads those 25, and the Classic Collection is not priced here.

There is a second Celebrations set and it is not on this page. The Classic Collection is 25 cards numbered CC001 to CC025, released the same day: reprints of older cards, CC001 Blastoise through CC025 Tapu Lele GX. The card databases this site reads file it as a set of its own, so none of it is in the chase cards, on the checklist, or in any total anywhere on this page, and we hold no price for a single one of those 25 cards. Quick facts

Celebrations 101

  • Released October 8, 2021, which was 4 years ago.
  • 4 of the 25 cards sit at a chase rarity, which is every tier highlighted in the rarity breakdown below. That is what the checklist holds, not how often any of it turns up.
  • The chase card of the 25 on this checklist is Mew (Secret Rare), sitting around $55.96 raw. The 25 cards of Celebrations: Classic Collection are not on it, and this site holds no price for any of them, so there is no honest way for this page to tell you whether one of those beats it.
  • We have ripped 1 video worth of this set on the channel.
  • 25 cards makes this the smallest of the 28 English sets on this site, and one pack costs about a third of what buying one of every card costs. Both of those figures are further up this page.

Cost per pack is the market price divided by the pack count printed on each card above, so you can check it. Sleeves, dice, decks and promo cards are counted as worth nothing, which flatters every box that includes them. Anything whose pack count is not in our data gets no per-pack figure rather than a guessed one, which is why blisters, tins and collection boxes have none, and why sets from before 2023 have none either: our counts are sourced for the current era and were not the same in earlier ones.
