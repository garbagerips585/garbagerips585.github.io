Quick facts

Chilling Reign 101

  • Released June 18, 2021, which was 5 years ago.
  • The set is 198 cards on paper, but there are 233 in total. The extra 35 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 82 of the 233 cards sit at a chase rarity, which is every tier highlighted in the rarity breakdown below. That is what the checklist holds, not how often any of it turns up.
  • The chase card is Blaziken VMAX (Secret Rare), sitting around $350 raw.
  • We have ripped 1 video worth of this set on the channel.
  • Three of the 35 Secret Rares in Chilling Reign are basic Energy cards. Water, Psychic and Fighting, the plainest cards in the game, sitting at the top rarity in the set.
  • 59 of the 233 cards are Trainers and 6 are Energy, so more than a quarter of the set is not a Pokemon at all.
