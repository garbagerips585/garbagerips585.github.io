Quick facts

When did Pitch Black come out, and how big is it?

  • Released July 17, 2026, which was 2 months ago.
  • The set is 84 cards on paper, but there are 120 in total. The extra 36 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 36 of the 120 cards sit at a chase rarity, which is every tier tagged CHASE in the rarity breakdown below. That is what the checklist holds, not how often any of it turns up.
  • Only 1 Mega Hyper Rare exists in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Mega Darkrai ex (Special Illustration Rare), sitting around $158 raw, and $769 in a PSA 10.
  • We have ripped 27 videos worth of this set on the channel.
  • Pitch Black is the newest of the 29 English sets on this site. Prices on a set this young move hard and fast, so treat every figure on this page as a snapshot rather than a settled number.

That PSA 10 figure is graded sales data from pricecharting.com, read October 8, 2026. The raw figure beside it is pricecharting.com's price guide value for an ungraded copy, which is a different measurement.

What is actually rare

Pitch Black secret rares and rarity breakdown

Pitch Black has 36 cards numbered past the printed set, #085 to #120, against a printed total of 84. That is what collectors mean by a secret rare: the number runs past the total printed beside it. Each one's printed rarity is on the ladder below.
