Quick facts

Pitch Black 101

  • Released July 17, 2026, which was 3 weeks ago.
  • The set is 84 cards on paper, but there are 120 in total. The extra 36 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 36 of the 120 cards are Ultra Rare or better. That is the slice of the checklist worth pulling.
  • Only 1 Mega Hyper Rare exists in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Mega Darkrai ex (Special Illustration Rare), sitting around $233 raw.
