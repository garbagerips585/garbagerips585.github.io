Quick facts

Pitch Black 101

  • Released July 17, 2026, which was 5 weeks ago.
  • The set is 84 cards on paper, but there are 120 in total. The extra 36 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 36 of the 120 cards sit at a chase rarity, which is every tier highlighted in the rarity breakdown below. That is what the checklist holds, not how often any of it turns up.
  • Only 1 Mega Hyper Rare exists in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Mega Darkrai ex (Special Illustration Rare), sitting around $249 raw, and $2,700 in a PSA 10.
  • We have ripped 24 videos worth of this set on the channel.
  • Pitch Black is the newest of the 28 English sets on this site. Prices on a set this young move hard and fast, so treat every figure on this page as a snapshot rather than a settled number.

That PSA 10 figure is graded sales data from pricecharting.com, read August 13, 2026. The raw figure beside it is pricecharting.com's price guide value for an ungraded copy, which is a different measurement read on a different day.

Cost per pack is the market price divided by the pack count printed on each card above, so you can check it. Anything whose pack count is not in our data gets no per-pack figure rather than a guessed one, which is why blisters, tins and collection boxes have none, and why sets from before 2023 have none either: our counts are sourced for the current era and were not the same in earlier ones.
