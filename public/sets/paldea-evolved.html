Quick facts

Paldea Evolved 101

  • Released June 9, 2023, which was 3 years ago.
  • The set is 193 cards on paper, but there are 279 in total. The extra 86 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 86 of the 279 cards sit at a chase rarity, which is every tier highlighted in the rarity breakdown below. That is what the checklist holds, not how often any of it turns up.
  • Only 9 Hyper Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Magikarp (Illustration Rare), sitting around $389 raw.
  • We have ripped 1 video worth of this set on the channel.
