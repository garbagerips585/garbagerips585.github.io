Quick facts

Perfect Order 101

  • Released March 27, 2026, which was 4 months ago.
  • The set is 88 cards on paper, but there are 124 in total. The extra 36 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 36 of the 124 cards are Ultra Rare or better. That is the slice of the checklist worth pulling.
  • Only 1 Mega Hyper Rare exists in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Mega Zygarde ex (Mega Hyper Rare), sitting around $133 raw.
  • We have ripped 44 videos worth of this set on the channel.
