Same cards, different name

Clay Burst is Paldea Evolved

If you have watched these rips and then gone looking for the set in a US shop, this is the one you want. Clay Burst is the Korean printing behind English Paldea Evolved, which English built by merging it with Snow Hazard (스노해저드, SV2P).

That is the symbol on an English Paldea Evolved card, printed at the bottom beside the collector number. It is not the mark on the Korean cards in these rips: the two printings carry their own.

The Korean printing of the Japanese Clay Burst. Clay Burst and Snow Hazard together became English Paldea Evolved. This is the set with the Iono chase card.
