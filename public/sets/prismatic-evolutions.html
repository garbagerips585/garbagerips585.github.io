Quick facts

Prismatic Evolutions 101

  • Released January 17, 2025, which was 19 months ago.
  • The set is 131 cards on paper, but there are 180 in total. The extra 49 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 55 of the 180 cards sit at a chase rarity, which is every tier highlighted in the rarity breakdown below. That is what the checklist holds, not how often any of it turns up.
  • Only 5 Hyper Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Umbreon ex (Special Illustration Rare), sitting around $1,420 raw, and $6,150 in a PSA 10.
  • We have ripped 13 videos worth of this set on the channel.
  • All eight Eeveelutions have an ex card here, and every one of them exists twice: a Double Rare worth a few dollars, and a Special Illustration Rare worth hundreds. Same Pokemon, same name, same set, and both are in the checklist below.
  • 32 of the 180 cards are Special Illustration Rares. No other set on this site has more than 22.

That PSA 10 figure is pokemonpricetracker.com's graded sales data, read August 12, 2026. The raw figure beside it is pricecharting.com's price guide value for an ungraded copy, which is a different feed and a different measurement.

The stars beside a tier are the ones printed on the card, redrawn from the key in the booklet that ships inside a modern set. The color carries as much of the meaning as the count: two silver stars is Ultra Rare and two black stars is Double Rare.
