Quick facts

Prismatic Evolutions 101

  • Released January 17, 2025, which was 18 months ago.
  • The set is 131 cards on paper, but there are 180 in total. The extra 49 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 55 of the 180 cards are Ultra Rare or better. That is the slice of the checklist worth pulling.
  • Only 5 Hyper Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Umbreon ex (Special Illustration Rare), sitting around $1,480 raw.
