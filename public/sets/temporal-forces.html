Quick facts

When did Temporal Forces come out, and how big is it?

  • Released March 22, 2024, which was 2 years ago.
  • The set is 162 cards on paper, but there are 218 in total. The extra 56 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 63 of the 218 cards sit at a chase rarity, which is every tier tagged CHASE in the rarity breakdown below. That is what the checklist holds, not how often any of it turns up.
  • Only 6 Hyper Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Gastly (Illustration Rare), sitting around $89.99 raw, and $470 in a PSA 10.
  • We have ripped 2 videos worth of this set on the channel.
  • The most expensive card in Temporal Forces is a Gastly, and it is an Illustration Rare rather than the top rarity in the set. Every Special Illustration Rare here is worth less than it.

A Pokemon Center figure is that exact product's price in Pokemon's own shop, read by hand on August 17, 2026. Pokemon Center is the manufacturer selling its own product, so this site treats its price as the suggested retail price: what sealed Pokemon should cost. The multiple beside it is the market price divided by it, and its name (about retail to 1.15x, a shop's margin to 1.5x, scarcity pricing to 2x, double and up past 2x) is this site's rule of thumb, not a measurement.

Cost per pack is the market price divided by the pack count printed on each card above, so you can check it. Sleeves, dice, decks and promo cards are counted as worth nothing, which flatters every box that includes them. Anything whose pack count is not in our data gets no per-pack figure rather than a guessed one, which is why blisters, tins and collection boxes have none, and why sets from before 2023 have none either: our counts are sourced for the current era and were not the same in earlier ones.
