Quick facts

Temporal Forces 101

  • Released March 22, 2024, which was 2 years ago.
  • The set is 162 cards on paper, but there are 218 in total. The extra 56 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 63 of the 218 cards sit at a chase rarity, which is every tier highlighted in the rarity breakdown below. That is what the checklist holds, not how often any of it turns up.
  • Only 6 Hyper Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Gastly (Illustration Rare), sitting around $93.22 raw, and $578 in a PSA 10.
  • We have ripped 2 videos worth of this set on the channel.
  • The most expensive card in Temporal Forces is a Gastly, and it is an Illustration Rare rather than the top rarity in the set. Every Special Illustration Rare here is worth less than it.

That PSA 10 figure is graded sales data from pricecharting.com, read August 28, 2026. The raw figure beside it is pricecharting.com's price guide value for an ungraded copy, which is a different measurement read on a different day.
