Same cards, different name

Violet ex is Scarlet & Violet

If you have watched these rips and then gone looking for the set in a US shop, this is the one you want. Violet ex is the Japanese printing behind English Scarlet & Violet, which English built by merging it with Scarlet ex (スカーレットex, SV1S).
