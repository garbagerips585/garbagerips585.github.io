Quick facts

Mega Evolution 101

  • Released September 26, 2025, which was 10 months ago.
  • The set is 132 cards on paper, but there are 188 in total. The extra 56 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 56 of the 188 cards are Ultra Rare or better. That is the slice of the checklist worth pulling.
  • Only 2 Mega Hyper Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Mega Lucario ex (Mega Hyper Rare), sitting around $272 raw, and $688 in a PSA 10.
  • We have ripped 5 videos worth of this set on the channel.
