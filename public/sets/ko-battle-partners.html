Same cards, different name

Battle Partners is Journey Together

If you have watched these rips and then gone looking for the set in a US store, this is the one you want. Battle Partners is the Korean printing behind English Journey Together.

  • 🇰🇷 Korean • SV9

    Battle Partners

    132 cards • January 24, 2025

    The one on this page, checklist below

  • English • SV9

    Journey Together

    190 cards • March 28, 2025

    Out 2 months later

    Read the Journey Together guide

That is the symbol on an English Journey Together card, printed at the bottom beside the collector number. It is not the mark on the Korean cards in these rips: the two printings carry their own.

The set behind English Journey Together. Its chase is Lillie's Clefairy ex, #184. Team Rocket's Mewtwo ex belongs to Destined Rivals, not this set.
