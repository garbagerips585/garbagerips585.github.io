How close is it to Perfect Order?

"Same set" is nearly true and worth checking rather than taking on trust. Here is every rarity in both printings, side by side. The gold rows are the ones that match.

3 of the 8 rarities hold exactly the same number of cards in both printings: 1 Mega Hyper Rare, 6 Special Illustration Rares and 18 Ultra Rares. English Perfect Order is 7 cards bigger, 124 against 117.

Both counts are the rarities on the two checklists, from TCGdex, read August 16, 2026. A rarity is only counted where TCGdex labels it, so this table appears on a guide only when both sides label every card. It says nothing about how often any of them turn up in a pack, because nobody outside The Pokemon Company knows that.
