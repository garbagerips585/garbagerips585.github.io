Same cards, different name

Nihil Zero is Perfect Order

If you have watched these rips and then gone looking for the set in a US store, this is the one you want. Nihil Zero is the Japanese printing behind English Perfect Order.

  • 🇯🇵 Japanese • M3

    Nihil Zero

    ムニキスゼロ

    117 cards • January 23, 2026

    The one on this page, checklist below

  • English • ME3

    Perfect Order

    124 cards • March 27, 2026

    Out 2 months later

    Read the Perfect Order guide

That is the symbol on an English Perfect Order card, printed at the bottom beside the collector number. It is not the mark on the Japanese cards in these rips: the two printings carry their own.

Card for card

How close is it to Perfect Order?

"Same set" is nearly true and worth checking rather than taking on trust. Here is every rarity in both printings, side by side. The gold rows are the ones that match.

3 of the 8 rarities hold exactly the same number of cards in both printings: 1 Mega Hyper Rare, 6 Special Illustration Rares and 18 Ultra Rares. English Perfect Order is 7 cards bigger, 124 against 117.

Both counts are the rarities on the two checklists, from TCGdex, read August 26, 2026. A rarity is only counted where TCGdex labels it, so this table appears on a guide only when both sides label every card. It says nothing about how often any of them turn up in a pack, because nobody outside The Pokemon Company knows that.
