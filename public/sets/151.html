Where the value sits

Where the money is

Buy one copy of every card in 151 at market price and you would spend $2,030.80. Half of it sits in 8 cards. The other 199 come to $937.97 between them, which is $4.71 a card.

In plain terms: a handful of cards carry the set and everything else is bulk. That is normal, it is true of nearly every modern set, and it is worth knowing before you buy a box hoping to "get your money back".

It is not what a booster box is worth, and it is not the chance of pulling anything: nobody outside The Pokemon Company has pull rates, so you will not find any on this site.

Quick facts

151 101

  • Released September 22, 2023, which was 2 years ago.
  • The set is 165 cards on paper, but there are 207 in total. The extra 42 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 42 of the 207 cards are Ultra Rare or better. That is the slice of the checklist worth pulling.
  • Only 3 Hyper Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Charizard ex (Special Illustration Rare), sitting around $378 raw.
  • We have ripped 1 video worth of this set on the channel.
