Quick facts

151 101

  • Released September 22, 2023, which was 2 years ago.
  • The set is 165 cards on paper, but there are 207 in total. The extra 42 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 42 of the 207 cards sit at a chase rarity, which is every tier highlighted in the rarity breakdown below. That is what the checklist holds, not how often any of it turns up.
  • Only 3 Hyper Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Charizard ex (Special Illustration Rare), sitting around $361 raw, and $1,493 in a PSA 10.
  • We have ripped 1 video worth of this set on the channel.
  • 102 different people and studios drew the 207 cards in this set. The artist's name is printed on every card, bottom left, and following one is the fastest way to find more art you actually like.
  • The three Kanto starters and Pikachu all got an Illustration Rare, and those four sit in the chase grid at the top of this page without being the top rarity in the set. Art moves a price as much as rarity does.

That PSA 10 figure is graded sales data from pricecharting.com, read August 22, 2026. The raw figure beside it is pricecharting.com's price guide value for an ungraded copy, which is a different measurement read on a different day.
