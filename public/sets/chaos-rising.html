Quick facts

Chaos Rising 101

  • Released May 22, 2026, which was 2 months ago.
  • The set is 86 cards on paper, but there are 122 in total. The extra 36 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 36 of the 122 cards are Ultra Rare or better. That is the slice of the checklist worth pulling.
  • Only 1 Mega Hyper Rare exists in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Mega Greninja ex (Special illustration rare), sitting around $213 raw.
  • We have ripped 51 videos worth of this set on the channel.
