Same cards, different name

Mega Brave is Mega Evolution

If you have watched these rips and then gone looking for the set in a US store, this is the one you want. Mega Brave is the Japanese printing behind English Mega Evolution, which English built by merging it with Mega Symphonia.

  • 🇯🇵 Japanese • M1L

That is the symbol on an English Mega Evolution card, printed at the bottom beside the collector number. It is not the mark on the Japanese cards in these rips: the two printings carry their own.

The other half of English Mega Evolution, out the same day as Mega Symphonia.

How close is it to Mega Evolution?

"Same set" is nearly true and worth checking rather than taking on trust. Here is every rarity in both printings, side by side.

No rarity holds the same number of cards in both printings. English Mega Evolution is 96 cards bigger, 188 against 92. It was built by merging this set with Mega Symphonia, which is where the extra came from.

Both counts are the rarities on the two checklists, from TCGdex, read August 26, 2026. A rarity is only counted where TCGdex labels it, so this table appears on a guide only when both sides label every card. It says nothing about how often any of them turn up in a pack, because nobody outside The Pokemon Company knows that.
