Quick facts

Surging Sparks 101

  • Released November 8, 2024, which was 21 months ago.
  • The set is 191 cards on paper, but there are 252 in total. The extra 61 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 69 of the 252 cards are Ultra Rare or better. That is the slice of the checklist worth pulling.
  • Only 6 Hyper Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Pikachu ex (Special Illustration Rare), sitting around $296 raw, and $901 in a PSA 10.
