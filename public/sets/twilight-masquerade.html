Where the value sits

Where the money is

Buy one copy of each of the 225 cards that has a price in Twilight Masquerade at market price and you would spend $1,384.71. Half of it sits in 4 cards. The other 221 come to $680.73 between them, which is $3.08 a card.

Quick facts

Twilight Masquerade 101

  • Released May 24, 2024, which was 2 years ago.
  • The set is 167 cards on paper, but there are 226 in total. The extra 59 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 65 of the 226 cards are Ultra Rare or better. That is the slice of the checklist worth pulling.
  • Only 6 Hyper Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Greninja ex (Special Illustration Rare), sitting around $376 raw, and $821 in a PSA 10.
  • We have ripped 5 videos worth of this set on the channel.
