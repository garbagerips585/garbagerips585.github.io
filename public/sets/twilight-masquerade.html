Quick facts

Twilight Masquerade 101

  • Released May 24, 2024, which was 2 years ago.
  • The set is 167 cards on paper, but there are 226 in total. The extra 59 are secret rares numbered past the printed count.
  • 65 of the 226 cards sit at a chase rarity, which is every tier highlighted in the rarity breakdown below. That is what the checklist holds, not how often any of it turns up.
  • Only 6 Hyper Rares exist in the entire set.
  • The chase card is Greninja ex (Special Illustration Rare), sitting around $313 raw, and $787 in a PSA 10.
  • We have ripped 5 videos worth of this set on the channel.
  • Perrin is in this set three times: an Uncommon, an Ultra Rare, and a Special Illustration Rare that is the second most expensive card in the set. Same person, three very different cards.

That PSA 10 figure is graded sales data from pricecharting.com, read August 22, 2026. The raw figure beside it is pricecharting.com's price guide value for an ungraded copy, which is a different measurement read on a different day.
