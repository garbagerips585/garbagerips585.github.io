Every Pokemon TCG set, in order

All 174 English sets from 1999 to 2026, oldest first, grouped by era. Release dates and card counts come straight from the Pokemon TCG API.

174sets
20,482cards
17eras
28of these have a guide
Base Gym Neo Other E-Card EX NP POP Diamond & Pearl Platinum HeartGold & SoulSilver Black & White XY Sun & Moon Sword & Shield Scarlet & Violet Mega Evolution

Set names, release dates and card counts are from the Pokemon TCG API. Card counts show the printed set size, with secret rares beyond it listed separately as a plus. An "unlisted" note is the opposite case and only 2 sets carry it: SWSH Black Star Promos, numbered to 307 with 3 not yet in the API's list, and Scarlet & Violet Black Star Promos, numbered to 215 with 19 not yet in the API's list. Those sets are still open, so the numbering runs ahead of the catalog and the count here follows the numbering. Underlined sets have a full guide on this site. The site also holds guides for Japanese and Korean sets, which are not English releases and so are on no row here. Topps made its own Pokemon cards from 1999 to 2004, trading cards rather than game cards, and those are on no row here either: the Topps guide covers every one of them. Fan made reference. Not affiliated with The Pokemon Company or Nintendo.
