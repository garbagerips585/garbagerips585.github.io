Pokemon lore

Facts worth knowing about the 1,025 Pokemon in the National Pokedex. Every line on this page is worked out from the Pokedex data rather than remembered, and each one says where it came from, because a fun fact with no source is just a rumor with good timing.

The mascots

Rochester's unofficial Pokemon

This city's most famous meal is the Garbage Plate. Its most famous export, if we get a vote, should be a walking trash bag. We did not choose Trubbish and Garbodor because of a joke we made up: the Pokedex got there first and files them as the Trash Bag Pokemon and the Trash Heap Pokemon.

Trubbish is #568, a Generation 5 pure Poison type, 0.6m tall and 31.0kg. It evolves into Garbodor, which triples in height to 1.9m and puts on 76.3kg getting there. Trubbish has a capture rate of 190 against Garbodor's 60, so the bag is a great deal easier to get hold of than the heap.

They are two of the 83 Poison type Pokemon in the dex, and two of the 16 that are pure Poison with no second type. Calling them Rochester's Pokemon is our claim and nobody else's, but we would like it noted that The Pokemon Company named a trash bag and a trash heap, and we named a channel after a plate of garbage, entirely independently.

Did you know

Type combinations

Of the 154 type combinations across the National Pokedex, 21 belong to exactly one Pokemon. Tyranitar is the only Dark and Rock Pokemon there has ever been.

Source: pokeapi.co, read August 13, 2026

The most common type

Water is the most crowded type in the dex with 154 Pokemon. The rarest is Ice, with 48.

Source: pokeapi.co, read August 13, 2026

Heaviest

Cosmoem and Celesteela are tied as the heaviest in the dex at 999.9kg, about 9,999 times the 0.1kg shared by the 5 lightest: Gastly, Haunter, Flabebe, Cosmog and Kartana.

Source: pokeapi.co, read August 13, 2026

Tallest

Eternatus stands 20.0m. At 0.1m you would need about 200 of the smallest stacked up to match that.

Source: pokeapi.co, read August 13, 2026

Hardest to catch

76 Pokemon share the lowest capture rate in the dex, 3 out of 255, including Articuno, Zapdos and Moltres. At the other end, 87 sit at the maximum of 255.

Source: pokeapi.co, read August 13, 2026

Legendaries

71 Pokemon are flagged Legendary and 23 Mythical, which is 9.2% of the 1,025 in the National Pokedex.

Source: pokeapi.co, read August 13, 2026

Babies

19 Pokemon are classed as baby Pokemon, a category that did not exist until breeding arrived in Generation 2.

Source: pokeapi.co, read August 13, 2026

The biggest generation

Generation 5 introduced 156 Pokemon, more than any other. Generation 6 added the fewest, at 72.

Source: pokeapi.co, read August 13, 2026
