How this list grows

How to get on this page

There is no form, no fee and no application. Tell us you exist and we will look you up and put you on. This list is short because nobody has been asked, not because anybody was turned down.

Email your listing

  • Send it however you like. A message, a comment on a rip, a tag in a story, whatever is easiest. Email is the surest one: garbagerips585@gmail.com. It does not have to be you: if you buy from somebody good, or you watch somebody local, send them instead.
  • Tell us these four things. Your name as you want it printed. Where you are: Rochester, Buffalo, Syracuse, or the town if it is none of those. What you make: rips, singles, art, competitive, something we have not thought of. Every handle you want on the card, and your own site if you have one. That is exactly what a card on this page holds, so nothing gets lost between you telling us and it going up.
  • Attach your logo or channel art. Any size, any format, straight off your phone is fine. It is what makes your card here look like you rather than like a line in a list. Nothing is uploaded on this site: it rides along on the email.
  • We look before we list. Every name here is a real person and every handle here has been opened and checked, because a wrong handle sends somebody to a stranger. That is also why the list grows slowly: it moves at the speed of checking rather than the speed of asking.
  • It costs nothing and it buys nothing. No paid placements and no affiliate links, on this page or anywhere on this site. The order is alphabetical and it always will be, so nobody can pay or ask their way up it.
