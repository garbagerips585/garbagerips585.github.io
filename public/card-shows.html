Never been to one?

What actually happens at a card show

A room of tables, each one somebody's stock. You can buy, you can sell, and you can trade, at the same table, in the same visit. Nobody minds which one you are there for.

  • Buy, sell or trade. All three, and you do not have to decide before you walk in. Bringing cards to sell is as normal as bringing money to spend.
  • Sealed, singles and graded. Booster boxes and packs, loose cards out of binders and cases, and slabs. Which of the three a table carries varies table to table.
  • Every vendor is different, so ask them. Walk up and ask what they have and what they are after. That is the whole etiquette, and it is how you find the person holding the thing you want.
  • 46 of the 61 coming up are free to walk into. Where a show has not published a price we say so rather than guess, so check the listing before you head out.

Card show 101: how it all works →

What the marks mean

Most of these are general card shows: sports, Pokemon and other TCG on the same floor. A show marked Pokemon show is a Pokemon event: Pokemon is what it is billed as and there are no sports, though a few also carry Magic or One Piece. One marked Pokemon here too is a general show where we have confirmed Pokemon is on the floor, either because we go to it or because the organiser says so.

A show marked Pokemon not confirmed is one we have not been able to check. Shows tend to say so when they are one thing only, so a general collectors show usually does have a mix, but that is a rule of thumb and not a promise. We are asking the organisers, and marks change as answers come back. Know a show we are missing, or can you confirm one? Tell us →
