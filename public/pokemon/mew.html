Evolution

Mew does not evolve

It has no pre-evolution and nothing it becomes, which puts it with the 200 other species in the Pokedex that stand alone. The evolution chart lists every one of them.

Evolution line from the National Pokedex, pokeapi.co, read August 13, 2026.

Every Mew we price

Prices are pricecharting.com's price guide value for an ungraded copy, read August 16, 2026.
