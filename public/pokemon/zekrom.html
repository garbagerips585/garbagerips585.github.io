Home / Pokemon / Zekrom

  • The Deep Black Pokemon. Zekrom is #644 in the National Pokedex.
  • Types. Dragon and Electric in the video games. A card only ever carries one type, so which one it is printed as depends on the card.
  • Generation 5, Unova. Flagged Legendary in the Pokedex.
  • Size. 2.9m (9ft 6in) and 345.0kg (761lb).
  • Capture rate 3 of 255. One of the hardest things in the games to catch.

Evolution

Zekrom does not evolve

It has no pre-evolution and nothing it becomes, which puts it with the 200 other species in the Pokedex that stand alone. The evolution chart lists every one of them.
