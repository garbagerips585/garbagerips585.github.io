Home / Pokemon / Rotom

  • The Plasma Pokemon. Rotom is #479 in the National Pokedex.
  • Types. Electric and Ghost in the video games. A card only ever carries one type, so which one it is printed as depends on the card.
  • Generation 4, Sinnoh. One of the 107 species that generation introduced.
  • Size. 0.3m (1ft 0in) and 0.3kg (0.7lb).
  • Capture rate 45 of 255. Middling, as capture rates go.

Evolution

Rotom does not evolve

It has no pre-evolution and nothing it becomes, which puts it with the 200 other species in the Pokedex that stand alone.
