Home / Pokemon / Vulpix

  • The Fox Pokemon. Vulpix is #37 in the National Pokedex.
  • Type. Fire in the video games, which is printed as Fire on a card. The eleven card types.
  • Generation 1, Kanto. One of the 151 species that generation introduced.
  • Size. 0.6m (2ft 0in) and 9.9kg (22lb).
  • Capture rate 190 of 255. About as easy to catch as the games get.
  • First English card. Base, January 9, 1999.
