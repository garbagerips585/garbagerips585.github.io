Every Cherrim card we could find. We hold no price for any of them, so this page names and pictures the printings instead.

Home / Pokemon / Cherrim

  • The Blossom Pokemon. Cherrim is #421 in the National Pokedex.
  • First English card. Diamond & Pearl, May 1, 2007.
  • Type. Grass in the video games, which is printed as Grass on a card. The eleven card types.
  • Generation 4, Sinnoh. One of the 107 species that generation introduced.
  • Size. 0.5m (1ft 8in) and 9.3kg (21lb).
  • Capture rate 75 of 255. Higher is easier to catch. This one sits in the middle.
