Home / Pokemon / Mimikyu

  • The Disguise Pokemon. Mimikyu is #778 in the National Pokedex.
  • Types. Ghost and Fairy in the video games. A card only ever carries one type, so which one it is printed as depends on the card.
  • Generation 7, Alola. One of the 88 species that generation introduced.
  • Size. 0.2m (0ft 8in) and 0.7kg (1.5lb).
  • Capture rate 45 of 255. Middling, as capture rates go.
  • First English card. SM Black Star Promos, February 3, 2017.

Evolution

Mimikyu does not evolve

It has no pre-evolution and nothing it becomes, which puts it with the 200 other species in the Pokedex that stand alone.
