Home / Pokemon / Palkia

  • The Spatial Pokemon. Palkia is #484 in the National Pokedex.
  • First English card. Diamond & Pearl, May 1, 2007.
  • Types. Water and Dragon in the video games. A card only ever carries one type, so which one it is printed as depends on the card.
  • Generation 4, Sinnoh. Flagged Legendary in the Pokedex.
  • Size. 4.2m (13ft 9in) and 336.0kg (741lb).
  • Capture rate 3 of 255. Higher is easier to catch. This is one of the hardest things in the games to catch.

Evolution

Palkia does not evolve

It has no pre-evolution and nothing it becomes, which puts it with the 200 other species in the Pokedex that stand alone. The evolution chart lists every one of them.
