  • The Eon Pokemon. Latias is #380 in the National Pokedex.
  • First English card. Nintendo Black Star Promos, October 1, 2003.
  • Types. Dragon and Psychic in the video games. A card only ever carries one type, so which one it is printed as depends on the card.
