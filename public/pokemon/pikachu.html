Home / Pokemon / Pikachu
  • The Mouse Pokemon. Pikachu is #25 in the National Pokedex.
  • First English card. Base, January 9, 1999.
  • Type. Electric in the video games, which is printed as Lightning on a card. The eleven card types.
  • Generation 1, Kanto. One of the 151 species that generation introduced.
  • Size. 0.4m (1ft 4in) and 6.0kg (13lb).
  • Capture rate 190 of 255. Higher is easier to catch. This is about as easy to catch as the games get.
