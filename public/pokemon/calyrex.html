Home / Pokemon / Calyrex

  • The King Pokemon. Calyrex is #898 in the National Pokedex.
  • Types. Psychic and Grass in the video games. A card only ever carries one type, so which one it is printed as depends on the card.
  • Generation 8, Galar. Flagged Legendary in the Pokedex.
  • Size. 1.1m (3ft 7in) and 7.7kg (17lb).
  • Capture rate 3 of 255. One of the hardest things in the games to catch.
  • First English card. SWSH Black Star Promos, November 15, 2019.

Evolution

Calyrex does not evolve

It has no pre-evolution and nothing it becomes, which puts it with the 200 other species in the Pokedex that stand alone.
