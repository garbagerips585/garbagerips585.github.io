Home / Pokemon / Regirock

  • The Rock Peak Pokemon. Regirock is #377 in the National Pokedex.
  • First English card. Hidden Legends, June 1, 2004.
  • Type. Rock in the video games, which is printed as Fighting on a card. The eleven card types.
  • Generation 3, Hoenn. Flagged Legendary in the Pokedex.
  • Size. 1.7m (5ft 7in) and 230.0kg (507lb).
  • Capture rate 3 of 255. Higher is easier to catch. This is one of the hardest things in the games to catch.

Evolution

Regirock does not evolve

It has no pre-evolution and nothing it becomes, which puts it with the 200 other species in the Pokedex that stand alone. The evolution chart lists every one of them.
