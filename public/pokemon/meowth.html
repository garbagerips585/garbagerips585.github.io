  • The Scratch Cat Pokemon. Meowth is #52 in the National Pokedex.
  • First English card. Jungle, June 16, 1999.
  • Type. Normal in the video games, which is printed as Colorless on a card. The eleven card types.
  • Generation 1, Kanto. One of the 151 species that generation introduced.
  • Size. 0.4m (1ft 4in) and 4.2kg (9.3lb).
