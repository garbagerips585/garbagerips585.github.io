  • The Flame Pokemon. Charizard is #6 in the National Pokedex.
  • First English card. Base, January 9, 1999.
  • Types. Fire and Flying in the video games. A card only ever carries one type, so which one it is printed as depends on the card.
  • Generation 1, Kanto. One of the 151 species that generation introduced.
  • Size. 1.7m (5ft 7in) and 90.5kg (200lb).
