Home / Pokemon / Lapras

  • The Transport Pokemon. Lapras is #131 in the National Pokedex.
  • First English card. Fossil, October 10, 1999.
  • Types. Water and Ice in the video games. A card only ever carries one type, so which one it is printed as depends on the card.
  • Generation 1, Kanto. One of the 151 species that generation introduced.
  • Size. 2.5m (8ft 2in) and 220.0kg (485lb).
  • Capture rate 45 of 255. Higher is easier to catch. This one sits in the middle.

Evolution

Lapras does not evolve

It has no pre-evolution and nothing it becomes, which puts it with the 200 other species in the Pokedex that stand alone. The evolution chart lists every one of them.
