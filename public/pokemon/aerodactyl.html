Home / Pokemon / Aerodactyl

  • The Fossil Pokemon. Aerodactyl is #142 in the National Pokedex.
  • Types. Rock and Flying in the video games. A card only ever carries one type, so which one it is printed as depends on the card.
  • Generation 1, Kanto. One of the 151 species that generation introduced.
  • Size. 1.8m (5ft 11in) and 59.0kg (130lb).
  • Capture rate 45 of 255. Middling, as capture rates go.
  • First English card. Fossil, October 10, 1999.

Evolution

Aerodactyl does not evolve

It has no pre-evolution and nothing it becomes, which puts it with the 200 other species in the Pokedex that stand alone.
