Every Nidoran♂ card we could find, what the ones we price are worth, and everything else worth knowing about Nidoran♂.

Home / Pokemon / Nidoran♂

  • The Poison Pin Pokemon. Nidoran♂ is #32 in the National Pokedex.
  • Type. Poison in the video games, which is printed as Darkness on a card. The eleven card types.
  • Generation 1, Kanto. One of the 151 species that generation introduced.
  • Size. 0.5m (1ft 8in) and 9.0kg (20lb).
  • Capture rate 235 of 255. About as easy to catch as the games get.
  • First English card. Base, January 9, 1999.
