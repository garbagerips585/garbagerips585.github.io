Home / Pokemon / Umbreon

  • The Moonlight Pokemon. Umbreon is #197 in the National Pokedex.
  • Type. Dark in the video games, which is printed as Darkness on a card. The eleven card types.
  • Generation 2, Johto. One of the 100 species that generation introduced.
  • Size. 1.0m (3ft 3in) and 27.0kg (60lb).
  • Capture rate 45 of 255. Middling, as capture rates go.
