  • The Feeling Pokemon. Ralts is #280 in the National Pokedex.
  • First English card. Ruby & Sapphire, July 1, 2003.
  • Types. Psychic and Fairy in the video games. A card only ever carries one type, so which one it is printed as depends on the card.
  • Generation 3, Hoenn. One of the 135 species that generation introduced.
  • Size. 0.4m (1ft 4in) and 6.6kg (15lb).
  • Capture rate 235 of 255. Higher is easier to catch. This is about as easy to catch as the games get.
