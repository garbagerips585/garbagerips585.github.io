About this game

Types, evolutions, Legendaries and the official categories, which are stranger than you remember. Every question is generated from the National Pokedex rather than written from memory, so none of it is somebody's half remembered fact.

Where the questions come from

There are five generators and no typed facts. Each one reads a single field off the National Pokedex and turns every Pokemon that has that field into a question, which is how 1,400 of them exist without anybody writing any. It also means an answer here is wrong only if the Pokedex is wrong, and you can go and check.

The official category

Every Pokemon has an official one word category and most people have never read one. Anything sharing a category is kept out of the wrong answers, so exactly one of the four on screen can be right.

643 questions, from the genus field
“Which Pokemon is the Copperderm Pokemon?”

Evolution, backwards

Asked backwards on purpose. What does this evolve into has more than one answer on the branching lines; what evolves into this has exactly one.

310 questions, from the evolvesFrom field
“Which Pokemon evolves into Whirlipede?”

Type

Single typed Pokemon only. A dual type has two right answers unless the question spells out the order.

305 questions, from the types field
“What type is Rellor?”

Which is heaviest

Four real Pokemon, pick the heaviest, and only when the top two are far enough apart that it is a question rather than a coin flip.

97 questions, from the wHg field
“Which of these is the heaviest?”

Legendary or not

One Pokemon flagged Legendary in the National Pokedex against three that are not.

45 questions, from the legendary field
“Which of these is a Legendary Pokemon?”

The categories are the good part

Nearly half the bank is the official one word category, because it is the one nobody knows and it is frequently ridiculous. These four are real, and two of them are why this channel is called what it is.
