Garbage Run

Tap the screen, or press space, to flip. Eat the rubbish, dodge the Pokemon.

How it works. Tap anywhere, or press space. Every pack you eat is a point. Other Pokemon are out there too and touching one ends the run. Get to 100 and Trubbish evolves into Garbodor for the rest of the game.
Nothing is saved anywhere but your own phone, and there is no account and no server. Your best score lives in this browser and goes away if you clear it.

The other games are quicker: a set guesser, a silhouette round and a trivia run. This one is for a longer wait.
