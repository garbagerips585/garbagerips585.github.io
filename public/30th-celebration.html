Pokemon 30th Celebration

The first Pokemon TCG expansion to launch on the same day worldwide in participating markets. It is all foil, there is no booster box, and every pack comes inside one of 15 products. This page is what is known about it, what is not, and how far along one master set is.

September 16, 2026Release, worldwide
199Cards in English, per PokeBeach
15Products, five waves
93Packs across them all

The master set, pocket by pocket

Nine pockets to a page, the same as the set's own Binder Collection. A filled pocket is a card actually in the binder. The empty ones are the job.

0 of 199 cards 0%

Nothing in it yet, which is correct rather than broken: the set does not release until September 16, 2026. Cards get added here as they are opened.

The 30 Pikachu 0 of 30

One in every pack, each by a different illustrator. Japan's first is Ken Sugimori redrawing his own Jungle Pikachu.

Main set 0 of 98

Everything else in the numbered main set.

Secret rares 0 of 33

Illustration rares, special illustration rares, and the two Futuristic rares.

Classic Collection 0 of 30

Reprints on gold bordered sparkle foil, numbered outside the main set. Not Standard legal.

Basic Energy 0 of 8

All eight are foil and all eight count. One comes in every pack.

199 is not an official number. PokeBeach's count of the revealed English list, 10 September 2026. The 30 Pikachu are part of the 128 main set cards. Several secret rares were still unrevealed: PokeBeach's own English gallery runs to number 158 and has four blanks in it, so part of the 33 is slots rather than cards anyone has seen. The Pokemon Company has never published a card count for this set, so these bars run against PokeBeach's count and may move when the last secret rares are shown.

What is actually in a pack

  • OfficialFive foil cards, one foil basic Energy and one Pokemon TCG Live code card.
  • OfficialEvery card in the set is foil, basic Energy included. Official.
  • Officialthe classic cards in 30th Celebration packs are NOT legal in Standard, but are eligible in formats that allow the original prints of those cards. Worth knowing before anyone builds around a Classic Collection pull.
  • OfficialPlayable in Pokemon TCG Live from 15 September 2026, 10am PDT, one day before the physical release, on iOS, Android, macOS and Windows, with login bonuses.
  • No boxThere is no booster box and no loose packs. Every pack comes inside one of the products below, so this set has no per pack retail price and a shop selling single packs is selling something else.

What every pack guarantees

THESE ARE CERTAINTIES AND NOT ODDS, which is why they lead. A guaranteed Pikachu in all 199 packs of a sealed run is worth more to somebody master setting this than any probability, because it is the one part of the set you cannot miss by being unlucky.

  • OfficialFive foil cards, one foil basic Energy and one Pokemon TCG Live code card in every pack. TPCi press release, 1 June 2026, footnote 2
  • OfficialOne of the 30 Pikachu in every single pack, each by a different illustrator. TPCi press release, 1 June 2026, and the pokemon.com product showcase
  • OfficialEvery card in the set is foil, basic Energy included. TPCi press releases, 1 and 30 June 2026
  • PokeBeachThe Ultra-Premium Collection's 30th pack is a Classic Collection pack holding three Classic Collection cards, on top of its 29 normal packs. PokeBeach's English product guide, 10 September 2026

One Japanese box, opened once

One box, not a rateThis is a SAMPLE OF TWENTY PACKS, not a pull rate, and it is a JAPANESE box. Japanese packs hold six cards where English packs hold five, and the two sets are different sizes (176 against 199), so it does not convert into an English expectation. It is on the page because it is the only opened-box data that exists for this set and because one opening of a known size is a fact about that opening. Do not restate it as a rate.

Free promo at the counter

A foil Mewtwo promo with $15 of Pokemon TCG purchases at GameStop, Best Buy, Hot Topic, Barnes & Noble, BoxLunch, Five Below and Macy's.

Cards the English set does not have

The Illustration Rares of Nidorina and Ditto are in the Japanese set and are NOT in the English one. Both were turned into English promos instead, which is why a Nidorina promo is the Elite Trainer Box's card.

Accessories

The Pokemon Center releases matching accessories on 16 September, the same day as the set: deck boxes, playmats, card sleeves, card displays, a card storage box and tote bags.

Japan's card list, all 173 revealed

This is Japan's set and not the English one. PokeBeach, 9 September 2026. The 30 Pikachu are part of the 103 main set cards. Japan's list and the English one are NOT the same set and the numbering does not line up: English has 128 main set cards to Japan's 103, because it folds in cards from Japan's Espeon ex and Umbreon ex 30th Celebration Premium Deck Set. It is here because it is the only full list of this set that exists in words anywhere: the English card images have been revealed but their names have not been published, so an English checklist would have to be guessed from Japanese numbers that demonstrably do not line up.

DERIVED, NOT REPORTED: Japan's revealed numbering runs #001-165 plus 8 Energy, which is 173 of the stated 176. Main is #001-103, then #104-135 is 32 secret rares, then #136-165 is the 30 Classic Collection. PokeBeach states 35 secret rares, so three are unrevealed. That is arithmetic on their own figures and is not a claim any source made.
