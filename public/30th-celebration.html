Pokemon 30th Celebration card list and set guide

The first Pokemon TCG expansion to launch on the same day worldwide in participating markets, on September 16, 2026. Every card is foil, there is no booster box, and every pack comes inside one of 15 products. Here is the full checklist with prices, every product and its date, and one collector's master set.

Cards the English set does not have

The Illustration Rares of Nidorina and Ditto are in the Japanese set and are NOT in the English one. Both were turned into English promos instead, which is why a Nidorina promo is the Elite Trainer Box's card.

Accessories

The Pokemon Center released matching accessories on September 16, the same day as the set: deck boxes, playmats, card sleeves, card displays, a card storage box and tote bags.

What is in a 30th Celebration pack

No odds needed for these: every pack guarantees them.

  • OfficialFive foil cards, one foil basic Energy and one Pokemon TCG Live code card in every pack. TPCi press release, June 1, 2026, footnote 2
  • OfficialOne of the 30 Pikachu in every single pack, each by a different illustrator. TPCi press release, June 1, 2026, and the pokemon.com product showcase
  • OfficialEvery card in the set is foil, basic Energy included. TPCi press releases, June 1 and June 30, 2026
  • PokeBeachThe Ultra Premium Collection's 30th pack is a Classic Collection pack holding three Classic Collection cards, on top of its 29 normal packs. PokeBeach's English product guide, September 10, 2026
  • No boxThere is no booster box and no loose packs. Every pack comes inside one of the products below, so this set has no per pack retail price and a shop selling single packs is selling something else.
  • OfficialThe Classic Collection reprints are not legal in Standard, though they are allowed in formats that permit the original printings (TPCi, June 1, 2026 press release).
  • OfficialLegal in Play! Pokemon Standard tournaments from September 25, 2026, except the Classic Collection (pokemon.com, September 16, 2026).
  • OfficialPlayable in Pokemon TCG Live since September 15, 2026, a day before the cards hit shelves. Logging in gets you a Mew ex Battle Pass deck, a Sylveon ex deck can be unlocked, and there are Missions for collecting all 30 Pikachu and all 30 Classic Collection cards (pokemon.com). How TCG Live works.

One large opening, not official odds

Not officialThe Pokemon Company has not published English pull rates (checked September 23, 2026). PokeBeach reports what TCGplayer found opening 3,000 English packs: about 1 in 21 packs held a Special Illustration Rare, 1 in 5 an Illustration Rare, 1 in 10 a Classic Collection card and 1 in 120 the Mew or Mewtwo Futuristic Rare. That is a large opening sample, not official odds, and it could not measure the RGB Mews.

What this channel has opened is counted separately and labeled as observed results, not odds. See those numbers.

What the cards themselves settled, and one Japanese box

Three cards we got early, on September 11, settle these straight from the printing.

  • Set code30C, printed on the Pokemon cards next to the regulation mark J.
  • 128 confirmed128. The cards print x/128 (Hydreigon 099/128, Igglybuff 120/128), which confirms the main set size from the cards themselves.
  • EnergyThe foil basic Energy are numbered in the separate MEE set (MEE 009 to 016), not in the main 128, though they carry the 30th Pikachu stamp.
  • Japan differsIgglybuff is #096 in Japan and 120/128 in English, and Hydreigon is not in Japan's set at all. The two lists do not line up.

One box, not a rateOne Japanese box of 20 six-card packs, opened once. It is one opening, not a pull rate, and it does not carry over to English packs, which hold five cards from a bigger set.

30th Celebration questions

When did 30th Celebration come out?
September 16, 2026, on the same day worldwide in participating markets, the first Pokemon TCG set to do that.
How many cards are in 30th Celebration?
199 by PokeBeach's count: 128 in the main set (30 of them Pikachu), 33 secret rares including the 3 RGB Mews, 30 Classic Collection reprints and 8 foil basic Energy. The cards themselves confirm the 128. The Pokemon Company itself only says "over 150 cards".
Is there a booster box?
No. There are no booster boxes and no loose packs: every pack comes inside one of the 15 products, from the September 16, 2026 Elite Trainer Box to the December tins.
What comes in a pack?
Five foil cards, one foil basic Energy and one Pokemon TCG Live code card. Every pack also has one of the 30 Pikachu.
What is the most valuable 30th Celebration card?
Lugia (#149/147), at $240.00 raw on PriceCharting, read September 23, 2026, among the cards with a published price. The RGB Mews are selling higher but have no settled price yet. The top ten.
Can you play the Classic Collection cards in tournaments?
The Classic Collection reprints are not legal in Standard, though they are allowed in formats that permit the original printings (TPCi, June 1, 2026 press release).
Are there official pull rates?
Not from The Pokemon Company yet. The largest count so far is TCGplayer opening 3,000 packs, which is a sample rather than official odds. More on that.
