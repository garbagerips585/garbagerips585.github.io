Pokemon 30th Celebration

The first Pokemon TCG expansion to launch on the same day worldwide in participating markets. It is all foil, there is no booster box, and every pack comes inside one of 15 products. This page is what is known about it, what is not, and how far along one master set is.

What is actually in a pack

  • OfficialFive foil cards, one foil basic Energy and one Pokemon TCG Live code card.
  • OfficialEvery card in the set is foil, basic Energy included. Official.
  • Officialthe classic cards in 30th Celebration packs are NOT legal in Standard, but are eligible in formats that allow the original prints of those cards. Worth knowing before anyone builds around a Classic Collection pull.
  • OfficialPlayable in Pokemon TCG Live from 15 September 2026, 10am PDT, one day before the physical release, on iOS, Android, macOS and Windows, with login bonuses.
  • No boxThere is no booster box and no loose packs. Every pack comes inside one of the products below, so this set has no per pack retail price and a shop selling single packs is selling something else.

What the cards themselves settled

From the printingREAD OFF THREE CARDS THE OWNER GOT EARLY, 11 September 2026, photographed on his own desk. This is PRIMARY evidence and it outranks every other source on this page: it is the publisher's own printing rather than a statement about it.

  • Set code30C, printed on both Pokemon cards next to the regulation mark J.
  • 128 confirmed128. Both Pokemon read x/128 (Hydreigon 099/128, Igglybuff 120/128), which CONFIRMS PokeBeach's figure of 128 main set cards from the printing itself rather than from a count of revealed images.
  • EnergyThe basic Energy is NOT numbered in the main set. It reads MEE 016, in the MEE Energy sheet, while carrying the rainbow 30th Pikachu stamp and YOSHIROTTEN's illustrator credit. So the eight foil Energy sit outside the 128.
  • Japan differsIgglybuff is #096 in Japan and 120/128 in English, and Hydreigon is in the English set and NOWHERE in Japan's 176 card list. The two sets are genuinely different and their numbers do not correspond, which is why this page does not map one onto the other.

What every pack guarantees

THESE ARE CERTAINTIES AND NOT ODDS, which is why they lead. A guaranteed Pikachu in every single pack of a sealed run is worth more to somebody master setting this than any probability, because it is the one part of the set you cannot miss by being unlucky.

  • OfficialFive foil cards, one foil basic Energy and one Pokemon TCG Live code card in every pack. TPCi press release, 1 June 2026, footnote 2
  • OfficialOne of the 30 Pikachu in every single pack, each by a different illustrator. TPCi press release, 1 June 2026, and the pokemon.com product showcase
  • OfficialEvery card in the set is foil, basic Energy included. TPCi press releases, 1 and 30 June 2026
  • PokeBeachThe Ultra-Premium Collection's 30th pack is a Classic Collection pack holding three Classic Collection cards, on top of its 29 normal packs. PokeBeach's English product guide, 10 September 2026

One Japanese box, opened once

One box, not a rateThis is a SAMPLE OF TWENTY PACKS, not a pull rate, and it is a JAPANESE box. Japanese packs hold six cards where English packs hold five, and the two sets are different sizes (176 against 199), so it does not convert into an English expectation. It is on the page because it is the only opened-box data that exists for this set and because one opening of a known size is a fact about that opening. Do not restate it as a rate.

199 is not an official number. 128 main set cards is CONFIRMED BY THE PRINTING: cards from this set read x/128. The rest of the 199 is PokeBeach's count of the revealed list, 10 September 2026 -- 33 secret rares, 30 Classic Collection and 8 basic Energy, with the 30 Pikachu part of the 128 and the Energy numbered outside it in the MEE sheet. Several secret rares were still unrevealed then: PokeBeach's English gallery runs to number 158 with four blanks in it, so part of the 33 is slots rather than cards anyone has seen. The Pokemon Company has never published a card count for this set, so these bars run against PokeBeach's count and may move when the last secret rares are shown.

Where the card pictures come from. 158 of the 199 come from TCGdex, which is where the rest of this site's card scans come from; it took this set two days after release. 38 are hotlinked from TCGplayer instead: the whole Classic Collection, because TCGdex holds those as a separate set with no images at all, and the eight foil Energy, which TCGplayer files under its own MEE set rather than under either 30th Celebration name. Nothing here is rehosted or resized. The remaining 3 have no picture anywhere this site may use: the 3 Mew RGB secret rares, which were revealed but which The Pokemon Company still has not acknowledged.
