TCGplayer market prices via TCGdex, read August 12, 2026. Where a card comes as a normal, holo and reverse holo at different prices, the figure is the priciest of them. 4,468 of the 4,481 cards from the sets we rip have a price. 13 do not, and they are English: TCGdex lists them with no TCGplayer entry at all, so they show nothing rather than a euro price converted into a guess. The remaining 35,226 printings sit outside the 23 sets we price. 16,290 of those are Japanese or Chinese, which have no US market price to quote in the first place, and 18,936 are English cards from sets we do not rip. Where a Japanese card could not be matched to a Pokedex number we show the name as printed and say so, because we do not invent translations. Singles move fast, so treat these as a ballpark and not a quote. We do not sell cards.
