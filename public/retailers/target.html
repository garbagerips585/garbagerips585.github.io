Does Target sell Pokemon cards?

Yes, and it is one of the two chains most people mean when they ask. Target files Pokemon under its own Toys & Games, Trading Cards department, and stocks the full sealed range.

Confirmed on August 17, 2026 by reading it on Target's own website: Opened two Target product pages for current Pokemon sealed product, both under the Target, Toys & Games, Trading Cards breadcrumb, and read the price on each. Open the page on target.com

What they stock

The bigger sealed formats too, so elite trainer boxes and booster boxes. The full sealed range including elite trainer boxes, three-booster blisters, booster bundles, tins and collections. Both Target-owned pages read on the day were sold out, which is the normal state of a Pokemon shelf at a big chain rather than a fact about Target.

Where in the shop to look

Target files them under Toys & Games, then Trading Cards, so the toy department. Target does publish an aisle label on a product page once you have picked a store, so if you want the aisle rather than the department, set your store on their own product page and read it there. It differs store to store, which is why there is no number here.

Where that was read. The breadcrumb on their own product pages: Target, Toys & Games, Trading Cards.

If the shelf is empty, ask

If you cannot see any, ask anyway. A lot of shops now keep trading cards behind the counter, in a locked case or in the back, and plenty do not put them on the sales floor at all. An empty peg is not the same as no stock, and people walk out of shops that had the thing every day of the week.

The question to ask is specific: do you have any Pokemon cards behind the counter. A staff member who has been asked ten times that morning will answer it in three seconds, and 'do you sell Pokemon cards' gets you pointed at an empty shelf.

What we have read them asking

Every figure below is one listing, of one product, on one day, at an address you can open and check. It is an example of the division rather than a score for the shop, and it will not be current for long. The suggested figures come from the MSRP page, which prices sealed product from Pokemon's own shop.

Sold by the shop itself: both Target product pages were reopened on August 17, 2026 and neither carried the 'Sold and shipped by' line that Target prints on a Target Plus partner listing, which is what marks them as Target's own stock. Every figure below was read on the product page, one listing at a time.

  • Mega Evolution Chaos Rising Three-Booster Blister. $14.99 asked against a suggested $13.99 for the Three-pack blister, so 14.99 ÷ 13.99 = 1.07. Read August 15, 2026. Open the listing on target.com
  • Mega Evolution—Pitch Black Elite Trainer Box. $59.99 asked against a suggested $49.99 for the Elite Trainer Box, so 59.99 ÷ 49.99 = 1.2. Read August 15, 2026. Open the listing on target.com

A price on their site is not always their price

Target Plus is Target's marketplace and its listings sit in the same search results as Target's own stock. A search for elite trainer boxes returned two-packs at $189.99 and $549.00 beside Target's own single boxes at $59.99. A partner listing carries a 'Sold and shipped by' line naming the seller; Target's own does not.

A price on target.com, walmart.com, bestbuy.com or amazon.com is not automatically that chain's price. All four are a shop and a marketplace at the same time: some listings are stock the chain owns, and some are independent sellers using the chain's website as a storefront and setting whatever price they like. The full explanation, and the three tells that separate the two, are on the shop list.

Getting hold of one

Purchase limits on Pokemon at Target are set per drop rather than as a standing rule. For one 2026 drop Target announced a limit of one unit per item per guest, in stores and online, on its own corporate newsroom.

Trading cards are final sale at Target. Their own return exceptions page names trading cards and trading card accessories, listing Pokemon, sport cards and binders, among items that cannot be returned.

Before you pay for it

Work out the multiple before you hand the money over. What a sealed product is supposed to cost has the suggested figure for every format and a calculator that does the division. If you are buying online instead, where to buy records what each venue costs once shipping and buyer fees are counted, and what recourse you have when something arrives wrong. When the chains are expected to restock is this week's drops.
