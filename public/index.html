The build order

Anatomy of a rip

A proper plate gets built in layers, bottom up, no skipping. Turns out a proper pack rip works exactly the same way.

  1. Home fries & mac saladThe base. The bulk commons. Every rip starts here and there is no shame in the base.
  2. Two burgers, always twoThe reverse holos. Not the dream, but they keep hope alive between packs.
  3. Meat hot sauceThe hits. Poured generously or not at all. There is no in between and we accept this.
  4. Mustard, onions, chaosChase card energy. Sometimes it is an alt art. Sometimes it is just onions.
  5. Bread & butter on the sideThe sleeve and the toploader. When you finally hit, you protect it.
