Cards, plates, and a Pokemon made of garbage

Garbage Rips 585 is one person in Rochester, New York, opening Pokemon cards on camera every single day. 365, no days off. Every rip starts the same way: wrappers into the Trubbish trash can on the desk, then we see what we get! Every pull gets celebrated, garbage ones included, and every pack gets tracked for the site.
