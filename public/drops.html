August 10, 2026 to August 16, 2026

What is dropping this week

Where Pokemon cards are expected to turn up, in store and online. None of this is announced by anyone. Retailers do not publish restock schedules, so every line here is people watching shelves and backend listings and comparing notes. Treat it as a decent guess, and check the confidence on each one.

5 in store, 3 online, compiled August 15, 2026 from community week-ahead post.

Pokemon CenterOfficial store Online Pattern only

A drop is expected, with no product list yet.

When. Usually Monday to Wednesday, 11am to 2pm Eastern.

Monday to Wednesday is a pattern people have noticed rather than a schedule Pokemon Center publishes, and nobody has measured this one.

Products showing on the backend do not mean stock exists. Nothing is confirmed until it is buyable.

TargetBig box In store Expected

Select Chaos Rising and Pitch Black products.

When. Through the week, varies by store.

Some stores are also still receiving Ascended Heroes booster bundles, Prismatic Evolutions surprise pack collections, and First Partner Series 2 and 3 collection boxes.

TargetBig box Online Expected

Prismatic Evolutions surprise pack collections, Pitch Black ETBs, Chaos Rising ETBs and First Partner Series 3.

When. No scheduled time. Any night from around 3am Eastern.

Loaded on the backend with no date attached, which means nobody knows when it goes live, including the people watching for it.

Best BuyBig box In store Expected

Ascended Heroes booster bundles, Pitch Black booster bundles and 3-pack blisters, First Partner Series 2 and 3 collection boxes.

When. Through the week, varies by store.

WalmartBig box Online Pattern only

Product list not published yet.

When. Wednesday evening is the pattern, most often between 9pm and midnight Eastern.

Walmart publishes no restock schedule. The closest thing to evidence is a measurement: Restockd, which monitors retailer stock endpoints, logged 83 Walmart Pokemon restocks in the 90 days to August 12, 2026 and found 87 per cent landed between 9pm and midnight Eastern, most often on a Wednesday.

That is a record of what happened, not a promise about what will.

No purchase limit in store, so these tend to go quickly.

How to read this page

What the labels mean. Confirmed: A date the retailer put on it, or stock already seen on shelves. Usual window: Expected inside a known range. Expected: The community thinks it is coming. No date. Pattern only: A day or time people have noticed. No retailer publishes a restock schedule, so this is the weakest thing on the page.

What the colours mean. The stripe on each name is the kind of shop, not the shop's own colour: Official store, Big box, Grocery, Pharmacy, Discount. Retailer names here are drawn in this site's own typeface and are the property of their owners. Nothing on this page is endorsed by, affiliated with or supplied by any of them.

Stock varies store to store and the good stuff goes fast, so this tells you where to look rather than what you will find. If you would rather buy without the hunt, the Rochester shops and the card shows have cards on a table right now, and pack prices shows what a pack actually costs before you pay a scalper for one.
