seller rather than a brick-and-mortar shop. If you are not sure which, use

=== HARD RULES ===

1. NEVER invent or guess a URL. Every link you write must be copied exactly
   from this message. If the page you want does not exist here, leave it out.
   A broken link in a description is worse than no link.

2. ALWAYS write "Rochester, NY" or "Rochester, New York" — never bare
   "Rochester". There is more than one Rochester in the United States and I am
   trying to rank for the New York one. The only exception is a proper name
   that genuinely contains it, such as "Visit Rochester".

3. Never call anything sponsored, affiliated, endorsed, or an affiliate link.
   Nothing on the site is for sale and no link carries a tracking code.

4. Do not promise a price in the description. Card values move. Say the guide
   has prices; do not state what a card is worth.

5. Keep my own voice and the existing structure of the description. The link
   block is an addition at the end, not a rewrite.

6. Do not add hashtags to the link block.

7. If the video has no hit and no set worth naming, still include the 📅 and
   🏪 lines. They apply to every video.

=== WHY ===

Every video should send viewers somewhere useful. The set guide matches what
they just watched, and the shows and shops pages are the local reason someone
in Western New York would subscribe.
